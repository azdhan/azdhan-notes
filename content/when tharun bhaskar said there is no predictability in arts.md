# tharun bhaskar 

The user listened to an interview of Tharun Bhaskar with Ideal Brain GV and Great Andhra Murthy. (during the release of remaked version of malayalam om shanti shanti shanti) In the conversation, Tharun Bhaskar spoke about the lack of certainty in gambling within the arts. He said there is no surety or predictability, and that excessive information still requires a leap of faith.

He emphasized that anomalies or breakthroughs cannot be predicted using existing data. To explain this, he recalled meeting industry experts who do predictive analysis for cinema, mentioning figures like Karan Johar. According to him, cinema involves around 700 variables to predict one or more outcomes. These are not constants but variables, which raises the question of whether something with so many variables can truly be called a prediction.

He also quoted the Dalai Lama, stating that the true purpose of an artist is to fulfill hope. In this context, he referenced several films and works that influenced or exemplified his thinking:

- _American Family Next Door_ (Netflix documentary series)
- _Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels_
- _Snatch_
- _Soodhu Kavvum_ (Tamil film)

He described _Keeda Cola_ as one of his best experimental films. He felt this because it broke conventional screenplay rules, particularly in structure, character introductions, character arcs, and the introduction of new characters in the middle of the film.

He also stated that anything which speaks to human beings in a universal emotional language can be called art.
