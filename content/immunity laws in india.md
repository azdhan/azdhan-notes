## election commission 
2013, modi regime [amended](https://frontline.thehindu.com/news/section-16-immunity-cec-eci-accountability-gyanesh-kumar-controversy/article71575308.ece) the act to give not only to the current ECs but also to the ex-ECs. why?  to disperse the duties in "unhindered and independent manner." in that case, one should ask: what is the exact thing that was stopping the EC to disperse their duties? 

now, we have to read this with how CEC election process has changed: earlier, the president (the rubber stamp of the PM) used to appoint the CEC, but after the 2023 SC [judgement](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/56882156/) that said a committee of PM+LOP+CJI should elect the CEC. but, the BJP regime changed it to PM+LOP+Union Minister selected by PM. this framework makes the PM choice as the de facto choice. when this amendment that is in force was challenged, the SC [delivered](https://www.scobserver.in/reports/sc-delivers-split-verdict-sends-eci-appointments-law-challenge-to-larger-bench/) a split verdict in sept 2026 and referred it to a larger bench.  

several NGOs and orgs have questioned this move, SC has [issued](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/the-debate-over-immunity-to-cec-ecs/article71589739.ece/amp/) the notice in oct 2026 for the centre to respond
