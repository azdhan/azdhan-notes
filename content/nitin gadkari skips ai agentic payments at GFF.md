  **# Nitin Gadkari skips AI agentic payments; launches OneTag for bank portability

Skipping everything related to AI and automated agentic payments, Union Minister for Road Transport and Highways Nitin Gadkari launched OneTag, a bank-portability feature for the existing NETC Fastag system at the Global Fintech Fest (2026), and announced a timeline for barrier-free tolling nationwide. OneTag lets users keep their FASTag sticker while switching to a preferred issuing bank.

What was missing from Nitin Gadkari’s session: According to the proposed agenda, his session at the Global Fintech Fest 2026, titled "India's Intelligent Highway: Tolling and the Agentic Future of Mobility Finance," promised a discussion:

“AI-driven, autonomous systems are transforming tolling and mobility payments into seamless, real-time financial experiences. This session highlights the convergence of infrastructure, payments, and intelligence to enable frictionless transit, smarter revenue models, and scalable mobility ecosystems for the future. A public-policy lens will frame the discussion around execution, ecosystem development, and wider economic impact.” - reads the description of the session

NPCI’s silence on agentic payments: While Gadkari never mentioned anything about AI or automated payments or agentic payments, it is important to note that NPCI was expected (according to sources [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/india-preparing-rollout-agentic-payments-upi-sources-say-2026-09-01/) spoke with) to launch its Unified Agent Protocol (UAP) at the GFF. Until now, the final day of the GFF, NPCI didn’t make any announcements or launches related to agentic payments. 

On the first two consecutive days of GFF, the agenda had an unnamed invite-only session related to the NPCI product launch. However, no announcement was made and no explanation was given. 

What is OneTag and how does it work: "Introducing OneTag, the new NETC Fastag portability feature. Keep the same NETC Fastag sticker on your car and switch to your preferred issuing bank anytime. The process is 100% digital and secure," the launch film shown at the event said. According to the short film, which looked like AI-generated, porting can be done through the Rajmarg Yatra app, with mobile verification, digital KYC, and wallet setup; the existing FasTAG continues to work, and future toll payments are processed by the newly ported bank.

No-toll-gate system and Multi-Lane Free Flow (MLFF): "Our next goal is to do away with stopping at toll plazas. In one year, we want all our tolls to move to Multi-Lane Free Flow (MLFF)... I feel 60–70% of the work will be done by December. By February-March, the entire country will have this [toll gate] barrier-free system," Gadkari said.

"This barrier-free system will work at 80 km per hour. No one will have to stop. Naturally, zero waiting time," he added

“In May 2026, highway mobility took its biggest leap with multi-lane free flow using automatic number plate recognition cameras and RFID. Mlff delivers barrierless tolling at highway speeds without stopping, and the evolution goes beyond the toll plazas,” read the voice over of an AI-generated-like clip on the 10-year journey of National Electronic Toll Collection (NETC). 

 The savings from Fastag: "The annual saving in time at toll plazas is 71 crore hours. Annual fuel saving is 56 crore liters, which is Rs. 5,250 crore. And the economic value of time saved is ₹68,500 crore per year," Gadkari said.
