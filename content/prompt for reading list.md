 

## Event context 

MediaNama is hosting an invite-only virtual roundtable, **“Technical Issues with SIM Binding”**, on **13 November 2026, 1:30 PM–4:30 PM IST (Zoom)**. The event examines the technical feasibility of the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) mandate requiring messaging platforms such as WhatsApp and Telegram to implement **continuous SIM–device binding**, making the physical presence of a registered SIM a mandatory condition for service continuity. The DoT directive was issued in November 2025; the compliance deadline was extended to **31 December 2026**, and the mandate is set to become operational from **1 January 2027**.

The discussion has two sessions:

1. **Technical Challenges with SIM Binding** — opening presentation by Meghna Bal on research findings; feasibility of SIM–device coupling; which layer performs the check (app, OS, or telecom operator); OS-level restrictions in iOS and Android; eSIMs and dual-SIM phones; multi-device sessions, desktop clients, cloud automation, enterprise APIs, CRM integrations, and AI agentic workflows; the Airtel and Vodafone Idea/Meta silent-authentication proposals and whether either satisfies “continuous presence”.
2. **Examining Fraud Prevention and SIM Binding** — evidence that SIM binding addresses India’s fraud and scam problem (phishing, caller-ID spoofing, OTP theft, SIM swaps, VoIP-based impersonation); how fraudsters might adapt; workarounds; safeguards and limits on excessive data collection.

The event page notes that MediaNama’s previous discussion on the [Impact of SIM Binding](https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-event-report-impact-of-sim-binding-on-social-media/) raised the technical impossibility of continuous SIM binding given modern OS architecture, while a recent CNBC report said Apple’s iOS had implemented changes required for SIM binding (no official confirmation from Apple or Google). Related MediaNama coverage cited: DoT’s November 2025 directive, the April 2026 deadline extension, the January 2027 operational date, and an assessment that SIM binding is “unworkable for apps”.
