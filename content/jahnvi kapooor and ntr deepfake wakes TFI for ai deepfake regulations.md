# Telugu celebrities call for AI deepfake regulations after viral explicit video of Janhvi Kapoor & Jr NTR

A recent  sexually explicit AI deepfake video of actress Janhvi Kapoor, involving her co-actor Junior NTR, has drawn strong condemnations from the celebrities calling for the AI deepfake regulations from the both state and central government. While a senior actor said that he’ll take this issue to the Telugu film chamber, a producer stated the telugu film industry intends to take this issue to Andhra Pradesh Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan.

After this incident, actress Janhvi Kapoor responded: 

"The criminal misuse of technology to objectify women and circulate fictionalised content without consent is deeply violating, bordering on sexual assault. Almost every woman in my industry has faced some version of this. And, women everywhere are increasingly being subjected to it." -- Janhvi Kapoor

Access a copy of her full statement from the screenshot of her Instagram story [here](https://th-i.thgim.com/public/entertainment/movies/vfekk1/article71546702.ece/alternates/FREE_1200/jahnvi%20story.png). 

Most of the links related to Janhvi Kapoor’s deepfake content circulating on social media are not active now. In November 2023, when a similar AI deepfake video of Actress Rahmika Mandanna went viral, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) [issued](https://www.medianama.com/2023/11/223-it-ministry-deepfakes-order-social-media-companies/) advisories to social media platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube, to take down deepfake content within 24 hours. Apart from this, according to the February’s amendments to the IT Rules, the intermediaries have to remove the AI generated or synthetic generated content within three hours.

However, as it happens with most of the viral content that is leaked/uploaded to the web, Janhvi Kapoorr's AI deepfake video is still available on a few certain porn sites. Some of them are already geo-blocked in India. 

In August 2026, Delhi HC [passed an order](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-delhi-hc-personality-rights-janhvi-khushi-kapoor/) to protect the personality rights of Janhvi Kapoor and her sister Kushi Kapoor. This included the takedowns of obscene or pornographic content. However, the judge refused to order a blanket takedown order. 

Jr NTR, the co-actor who is also the victim of AI deepfake in the same video, urged the state and central government to bring AI deepfake regulations. "This is a serious issue, and I urge all the state governments and the Government of India to act strongly and bring in strict regulations against the misuse of AI," he [wrote](https://x.com/tarak9999/status/2106292261843820830) on X. He is the first celebrity to call out the AI deepfakes.

Producer Naga Vamsi said that the industry will meet Andhra Pradesh Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan to address AI deepfakes: "Since this recent issue is such defaming and vulgar, we are taking a collective decision to speak with Kalyan garu to discussion what can be done from the Central government," he [said](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wytyWPyN-_A) at a different press meet. 

Actor Chiranjeevi, who is also the brother of AP DyCM Pawan Kalyan, said that "Central and State Governments to take strong and effective measures against the misuse of AI for such abuse." While tagging Telangana and AP CM and Union Ministers Amit Shah and Ashwini Vaishanaw, he also [said](https://x.com/KChiruTweets/status/2106420082192904559): 

"I will take this matter up with the Telugu Film Chamber, the Producers’ Council and our affiliated industry bodies, and recommend the formation of a dedicated committee and support cell. Working closely with Cyber Crime authorities, this team should support those affected, coordinate efforts to remove such content and pursue legal action against those responsible." -- Actor Chiranjeevi

"Whoever is responsible for this should not be spared. They must face the harshest possible legal consequences and be made an example of. We must not let this go... This is the digital equivalent of sexual assault. The law must treat such acts with utmost seriousness," [wrote](https://x.com/TheDeverakonda/status/2106367084032860288) actor Vijay Devarakonda.

One actor suggested linking Aadhaar ID to social media accounts. "Accountability is the need of the hour. Linking Aadhaar to online identities may be the most effective way to ensure accountability and curb online abuse going forward," [wrote](https://x.com/ramsayz/status/2106404873357144409?s=20) Ram Pothineni, responding to Jr NTR's post condemning the deepfake video. 

In September 2025, in an event unrelated to deepfakes, Telugu actor Sai Dharam Tej also [said](https://x.com/M9News_/status/2106428149135229384?s=20): "If possible, I really want all the social media platforms to have some sort of connection to the real world, like checking a social media account with an authenticated ID like Aadhaar Card or their parents or something." He made this statement in the context of making social media platforms safer for the children.
