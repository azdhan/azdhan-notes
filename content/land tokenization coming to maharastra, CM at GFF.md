 **Maharashtra is working on land tokenization act: CM Devendra Fadnavis at GFF

"We are now bringing into place the Architecture of Maharashtra Digitization and Exchange of Land Token Asset Act, that is the DELTA Act," [said](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbf3MUEBQnA) Maharastra CM Devendra Fadnavis at Global Fintech Fest 2026. He also stated that Maharashtra will soon become to legislate such land asset tokenization framework. 

"Our objective is to create a legal framework for the blockchain-based tokenization of land and other immovable assets, and we are doing it carefully. We are bringing together expertise from SEBI, [Business Advisory Committee (BAC), National Advisory Council (NAC), industry, technology, law, and academia because innovation at this scale must be accompanied by legal certainty, consumer protection, and regulatory trust," -- MH CM Devendra Fadnavis

  
Land tokenisation value could be Rs. 50 lakh crores:  "Last year, right here at the Global Fintech Fest, I had a fascinating conversation with my friend Kunal Shah. During that conversation, I made a commitment. I said Maharashtra would work towards becoming India's first tokenised state. I said that enormous value is locked in our physical assets, particularly land and real estate, and that technology can help us unlock this dormant capital. We estimated that this opportunity could be as large as Rs. 50 lakh crores," he stated before announcing the DELTA Act. . 

CM Fadnavis echoed [Nandan Nilekani's views on land tokenization](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-tokenization-land-assets-nandan-nilekani/). In March 2025, during a public talk at an event organized by Arkam Ventures, Nilekani said that India is currently unable to monetize its fragmented land assets and landowners can neither easily sell their land nor use it as collateral for credit. 

At GFF, Fadnavis said:

"For generations, land has represented wealth in India. But very often, it is wealth without liquidity. A family may own an asset of significant value and yet struggle to access capital against it efficiently. An entrepreneur may have an asset but may not be able to unlock its economic potential quickly." 

"Let me make one thing absolutely clear. For us, tokenization is not about speculation. It is about unlocking productive capital. It is about bringing transparency. It is about improving liquidity. It is about making assets more productive. And ultimately, it is about converting dormant wealth into economic opportunity." 

"The first generation of digital finance gave people access. The next generation can give the people agency."

 “50% of India’s wealth is actually in land. And this is even more so in rural India, where everybody has a small piece of land, maybe it’s fragmented, and there’s no way to monetize it,” -- [Nandan Nilekani (March, 2025)](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-tokenization-land-assets-nandan-nilekani/)

Here are some of the key statements on land tokenization made by other speakers: 

"Tokenization allows many different kinds of assets to be digitized, certified by a registrar, and transacted digitally, whether for money or other tokens, unlocking liquidity and transparency across asset classes that have historically been slow moving or hard to access," said   Kris Gopalakrishnan. chair person of GFF 2026 and co-founder of  Infosys. 

"Tokenization could transform the way value moves. It can bring money, assets, ownership, and the rules governing them into programmable infrastructure," said Sohini Rajola, Executive Director for Growth at NPCI.
