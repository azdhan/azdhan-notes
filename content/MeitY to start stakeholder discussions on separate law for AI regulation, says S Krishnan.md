




**"We will have to start discussing in various groups as to what the stakeholders feel about it [AI regulation] and we will start a process of drafting,"** [said](https://x.com/ians_india/status/2075111925269356943?s=20) S. Krishnan, Secretary at Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) during a brief press interaction in New Delhi at the Confederation of Indian Industry's (CII)  [GCC Business Summit](https://cam.mycii.in/OR/EventDetailOR.html?id=E000074278) in New Delhi on July 9 as reported by news agency ANI. Watch the full video of his brief press interaction with the media [here](https://www.youtube.com/live/PDp-GqAhyXM?si=8nTKb3s-e7lQYrc6).

As MediaNama reported earlier, in December 2025, MeitY’s Secretary S Krishnan [stated](https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-india-ai-law-digital-india-act-stalled/) that the government is not planning a new dedicated AI law. However, after six months later, while speaking in an interview with The Hindu, he [clarified](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-vaishnaw-new-ai-law-contradicting-meitys-position-2023-parliamentary-reply/) that the Ministry is in talks with the industry for the new AI law. " I do think that there is a requirement for a new law because the world of AI is very different from the world when the IT Act was enacted in 2000," he added. Explore more here: [Hindu's interview on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UXF88tB0j_0&t=17s) | [Medianama reporting](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-vaishnaw-new-ai-law-contradicting-meitys-position-2023-parliamentary-reply/) ] 

**"In total, about 762 such suggestions were received, and we are now working with the various ministries to determine how to take these forward,"** he further said referring to the submissions by various ministries on AI adoption and the types of applications they want, based on their specific requirements. 

"During the AI Summit, the Prime Minister had asked all ministries to visit the summit, listen to the various speakers, and als
o explore the stalls in the exhibition. Afterward, they were to return with suggestions on where AI could be applied within their respective ministries," he added.

Referring to the recent notices sent to WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram and possibly other messaging platforms, Krishnan said that they haven't received the responses yet from WhatsApp or others and they are still awaiting for their submissions as today (June 9) remains the last day for the response submissions.  

Addressing the [Meta latest "muse" feature](https://www.medianama.com/2026/07/223-meta-muse-image-public-instagram-photos-opt-out/), he said, "We will have to look at it with reference to legal framework and whether it is in accordance with legal framework or not. We will examine the representations we received."

When reporters brought up [Meta's recent blogpost on CSAM, ](https://www.medianama.com/2026/07/223-meta-defends-robust-ad-review-after-instagram-csam-ads/) in the context of recent BBC investigation,  Krishnan said that they haven't received the formal response from Meta and added that the government's view will depend of their response. He further affirmed that Meta still has time for the submission on the CSAM issue. The Ministry sent notice to Meta on the issue of allowing CSAM-related ads on its platforms.

He also responded that he doesn't have an update related to the recent data breach at Tata Electronics.
