# Bombay HC orders takedown of Nitin Gadkari’s AI deepfakes and seeks user data

The Bombay High Court (HC) ordered the takedown of AI-generated deepfake content related to [controversial E20 Ethanol-blended fuel](https://www.reporters-collective.in/trc/indian-motorists-paid-88-234-crore-extra-to-fuel-ethanol-experiment) advocated by Nitin Gadkari, Union Minister of Road Transport and Highways of India. The HC also ordered takedown of future content too, if it is flagged by Gadkari.

The court also sought the user data of all the account holders specified in Gadkari's plea. "Furthermore details of all account holders, the basic subscriber information of all individuals who have posted should be furnished," read the order reported by Bar and Bench.  You can read the full live reporting [here](https://x.com/barandbench/status/2084880129076142285?s=20). 

"I can only say that the same is absolutely vile and abusive. Such material should have no place on a public platform, which is accessible to everyone, including young. The applicant has made out a case for ad interim relief. Meta and Google have appeared and it is ordered to take down the material set out in exhibit c. " said the  single-bench judge Justice Arif Doctor

"No, look at the content. I am surprised you [intermediaries] are taking such a stand," reported Bar and Bench citing the HC. Justice Doctor made these remarks when Google's counsel argued that "He [Gadkari] may approach us with a complaint and if we do not take down, then he may approach the court."

Don’t you have a mechanism to take this down without parties having to come to court?" asked the court. "But with the technology at your disposal, don’t you have a policy that if it is patently abusive.. say someone uploads vulgar content?" HC further asked when Meta's representatives reportedly argued that there are "so many accounts and so many posts" at the designated grievance official. Gadkari's legal counsel argued back saying that "They [intermediaries] should publicize how to raise grievances."

What does Nitin Gadkari’s plea state: According to [LiveLaw's](https://www.livelaw.in/high-court/bombay-high-court/nitin-gadkari-e20-petrol-policy-defamation-takedown-ai-videos-544393) reporting, Nitin Gadkari's plea stated that Gadkari and his Ministry of Road and Transport had no role in E20 policy making. The plea reportedly also said that the then Central Government in 2003 had introduced Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP) as a national policy to be implemented in a phased manner.  

"The purpose and object of filing the Suit is not to curtail or prevent the public at large from engaging in discussion, debate, analysis or fair, just and bona fide criticism of any decision taken by the Plaintiff himself or by his office. The Plaintiff respectfully submits that he does not seek to stifle or restrain fair criticism, dissent, debate or bona fide expression of opinion concerning his public life, governmental policies or official functions. However, the Defamatory Content is ex facie false, fabricated, malicious, abusive and grossly defamatory per se, and constitutes defamation in law. Further, the Deep Fake Content exploits the Plaintiff's personality and publicity rights, without his knowledge, consent or authorisation," reported LiveLaw.
