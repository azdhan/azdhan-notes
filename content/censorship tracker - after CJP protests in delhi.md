# Censorship Tracker: A live list after Jantar Mantar Protests in New Delhi

*Last updated on 22 July, 2026 at 04:36 pm* 

The student protests at New Delhi's Jantar Mantar continue even after a day of severe police violence against civilians and an internet shutdown. Indian authorities have begun taking down or geo-restricting content through notices possibly under the Section 79(3)(b) and Section 69A of the IT Act. 

Here is the running documentation of content that Indian authorities doesn't want the public to see. If you are a victim of such censorship or knows someone whose content has been taken down or blocked arbitrarily, please email me at azdhan@medianama.com

- **Journalist at DW News,** Sadhika Tiwari's reel posted on 20 July, 2026 was geo-blocked in India. The post is features a young student crying after she was allegedly beaten by the police at CJP protest.

- Instagram is also restricting stories, not just posts and reels, claimed @Royalsinghz3 while [posting](https://x.com/Royalsinghz3/status/2079368760075727353?s=20) on X saying that Meta is "even taking down stories of small accounts who are speaking against CJP" You can see the alleged notice sent my Meta [here, on X](https://x.com/Royalsinghz3/status/2079368760075727353?s=20).
- @AnilSir_Uncut posted a cropped version of a Meta [screenshot](https://x.com/AnilSir_Uncut/status/2079231641608913122?s=20) saying "Your account can't be shown to non-followers." We couldn't validate this.  
- @cookykoyalogy [posted](https://x.com/cookykoyalogy/status/2079199291672068588?s=20) the Meta notices indicating the blocking of her account. "The account, or activity on it, may deceive others to take their money, property or personal information," stated Meta's notice, citing the community guidelines. Since the user had blurred the Instagram username, we couldn't validate it. "The Govt of India is shadow banning people to stop it from gaining momentum. Don't stop now. SpeakUp," wrote the user.
- **Content creator** Akshay Chandra Madhav [posted](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbFwnonImwM/?igsh=MWVmMG9xZ2hnb3Z4bg==) on Instagram, sharing the screenshots showing that Instagram had removed at least two of his posts for violating its Community Guidelines, while another post was withheld in India citing the Information Technology Rules. He said he could no longer access all the posts he had shared as Stories and urged archivists to preserve copies elsewhere to ensure there is a record. He also alleged that Meta was coordinating with the government to erase evidence.
