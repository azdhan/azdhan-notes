# X locked Times of India photojournalist's account after Delhi smog posts: Report

X appears to have briefly locked Times of India photojournalist Anindya Chattopadhyay's account after he posted images of Delhi's post-Diwali pollution and smog, according to Chattopadhyay’s report  in the [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/delhi/x-locks-toi-photojourno-account-after-smog-posts/articleshow/135698284.cms). Chattopadhyay said the platform cited a "violation of its rules" when it locked the account. However, at the time of writing this, the account was accessible. 

Chattopadhyay said it was his duty to show "the truth of the post-Diwali situation through my camera". He also wrote that X locked his account after the uploads:

“The scene there was no better. The city's iconic tombs were barely visible through the dense smog. Concerned by the conditions, I started uploading the photographs and videos to my X account…. My X account was locked after I posted photographs and videos showing poor visibility around NH9 and Lodhi Garden, with the platform citing a “violation of its rules”. – TOI Photojournalist.

[https://x.com/ANINDYAtimes/status/2119640359374602541](https://x.com/ANINDYAtimes/status/2119640359374602541) 

Government denies involvement

TOI reported that the Delhi government denied any involvement in the lock. The TOI report does not say whether any other authority sent X a request. It also does not say whether X has responded or restored the account.

What we don't know

- Which X rule the posts allegedly violated.
    
- Whether a human reviewer or an automated system took the decision.
    
- Whether any legal request, under the Information Technology Act or otherwise, preceded the lock.
    
- Whether the account has been restored.
    

We’ve emailed X asking these questions, we’ll update the copy once we receive a response.

How this compares with recent cases

Most account restrictions MediaNama has tracked in recent weeks cite a legal demand, not a platform's own rules. X withheld [Polymarket's account](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-polymarket-x-account-blocked-india/) in India with a legal-demand notice. Instagram restricted [Khurpench's account](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-instagram-restricts-khurpench-account/) under the IT Rules, 2021. The Chattopadhyay lock differs because X pointed to possibly its own rules or policies or community guidelines, since TOI’s report claims that there was no legal notice that stated the sections under which the account was locked. 

Recent censorship and takedowns in India

1. [Jammu & Kashmir's Kishtwar curbs](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-jk-curbs-fake-news-provocative-content/): The district magistrate imposed two-month restrictions on fake news and provocative content, including reaction videos. Officials must also compile a verified inventory of digital news outlets.
    
2. [Upper Siang shutdown](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-mobile-internet-shutdown-siang-dam-bandh/): Mobile internet was suspended in Arunachal Pradesh hours before a 36-hour anti-dam bandh began on November 2.
    
3. [Ila Kazmi's Instagram block](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-it-ministrys-blocking-takedowns-law/): The journalist's block raised questions about the use of Section 79(3)(b) and the role of MeitY's Section 69A Cell.
    
4. [Paranjoy Guha Thakurta's website](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-hostinger-paranjoy-guha-thakurta-website-newsclick-article/): Hostinger briefly took the site down on October 28 over an old NewsClick article. This came days after a Delhi court passed an ex parte order against the article.
    
5. [Rakesh Ranjan's account](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-rakesh-ranjan-cartoon-instagram-restricted-india/): Instagram restricted the cartoonist's account in India, citing repeated legal takedowns. The notice left key questions unanswered.
    
6. [LiveLaw's reel](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-instagram-restores-livelaw-reel/): Instagram restored the reel on an Election Commission of India affidavit after restricting it over a legal request. Who made the request, and under which law, remains unclear.
    
7. [Reddit's 26 posts](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-reddit-posts-india-china-border-order/): Reddit blocked posts on the India–China border on a government order that names no law. The order's sender is listed as "redacted".
    
8. [Polymarket's X account](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-polymarket-x-account-blocked-india/): The account was withheld in India with a legal-demand notice, five months after authorities restricted its website.
    
9. [Mumbai Police notice to X](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-mumbai-police-x-takedown-notice-modi-adani-video/): Mumbai Police asked X to remove 24 posts linked to Sabeer Bhatia's blocked video on Modi and Adani. The request followed a block of the video on YouTube.
    
10. [NIXI and chalosansad.in](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-nixi-website-neet-protest-live/): The National Internet Exchange of India removed a website documenting NEET-UG paper leak protests three days after it went live, without notice. The Delhi High Court has sought its response.
