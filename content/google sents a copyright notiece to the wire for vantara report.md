# Google sends a copyright notice to The Wire for reporting on Anant Ambani’s Vantara

Google has reportedly sent a copyright violation notice to The Wire against their own reporting on Anant Ambani's Vantara project. "Of course there is no “copyright violation” but someone out there wants to use this bogus charge to kill the story," [said](https://x.com/svaradarajan/status/2110283093680730396?s=20) The Wire's founding editor, Siddharth Varadarajan, on X.  

"Some people never learn. This is the route that those who are trying to censor news have started using. It doesn’t work and it only gives us an excuse to re plug the story and remind readers of the old dictum — News is what the powerful don’t want you to read."  -- Siddharth Varadarajan, The Wire's founder and Editor. 

This the concerned story that 'someone' had complained against: ["'Permanently End Wild Animal Imports,' 27 International Organisations Urge Vantara"](https://thewire.in/environment/permanently-end-wild-animal-imports-27-international-organisations-urge-vantara)   (Still live on The Wire)

What is the story about?  Building on top of The Wire's reporting on Vantara, Journalist Aathira Perinchery's story, published on September 24 2026, is about an  [open letter](https://www.prowildlife.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-09-22-organisations-respond-to-vantara.pdf) signed by various wildlife protection organizations.  The letter sought Vantara's Greens Zoological Rescue and Rehabilitation Centre to 'permanently end all its wild animal imports and provide a public inventory of all animals being held in its facilities,' as per The Wire's report.
