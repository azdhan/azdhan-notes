# a legal ethical drama on a death bed
a rural uneducated old villageman knows all the legal and ethical stuff he knows about the policy and rights, all through the cinema and youtube. he becomes more philosophical as he grows older and he finds the socrates-like debating companion in the neighbouring village, a small but young curious kid who is generally kicked out of any sociable event for his unfiltered but polite rebellious nature. 

as the kids of the villageman avoid and leave him, very naturally and understanably, the youthful guy becomes the everything for the lonely old man. he finds comfort in the internet and a companion in the young boy. 

when the oldman is in the bed-ridden and near to death eventually, after several years, the youngboy comes to the village and as the person who was very close to the oldman, in an intellectual way and a heartfelt-wholesome way (almost like sathyam sundaram types), he takes the initiation and the responsibility of him. the biological kids, the hypocrites who never really cared for their father claims more rights over the youngboy (now older). 

oldman somehow knows about [DNR](https://share.google/aimode/foRmL0XH4iij3SRwM) and euthanasia. he sorta begins to believe that hedonism is the only point of life in his own way. and, the only person who knows about this is the youngboy. the oldman cant write, cant read very much, only briefly. so, the only testement to the "Real" beliefs of the old man is the youngboy--who is a complete stranger to the whole village. the things that the youngboy tells about the old man to the remaining villagers/family are also as strange as he is to them. they all never know the old man to the depths that this youngboy knew. 

the youngboy stands and wants everyone to stand up for what the oldman wants/wanted to believe/stand for. 

thus the drama begins: whom does the "body" belongs to after a "point"? who should have the rights to take the decisions when the "body" cant take "its" own decisons-- the biological child who doesn agree/know what the oldman believees? or the non-biological kid who connects with the old man intellectually/emotionally/empathically?
