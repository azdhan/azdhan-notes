YouTube has taken down a video essay on Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Gautam Adani, titled: **"How Modi and Adani Stole India I The Friendship Files,"** which was created by Sabeer Bhatia, the co-founder of Hotmail. 

The [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XEK8QRXSKLM) was taken down within a few days after uploading, which was originally posted on October 9th and had only 45,000 views at the time of takedown. However, the video is accessible via VPN. 

The [YouTube notice](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HUVacPOakAABDPP?format=jpg&name=900x900) states that it had "received a legal complaint from a government entity" regarding his content. The note further said that that, **"after review,"** the video has been blocked in India. The notice also had a link out to a form if the user wants to raise a grievance. 

The video was uploaded in collaboration with: 

- **Bcubed** (Breaking Boundaries with Sabeer Bhatia), which has over 51.1k subscribers
- **Shadow Citizens,** which has 406 subscribers.

**Even after the blocking in India, the video essay went viral,** because the copies of the video has been circulating on social media**.** The video essay's creator, Sabeer Bhatia, has posted video on his X account that has now around over 1.8 million views. He also [shared](https://x.com/sabeer/status/2109474315578351910?s=20) the same video on Google Drive and on iCloud too. Later, various people have started sharing the video essay, including, Member of Parliament [Mahua Moitra.](https://x.com/MahuaMoitra/status/2109119142389584156?s=20) These duplicate copies are available to view in India at the time of writing this report.

 https://x.com/sabeer/status/2108347745614569888?s=20 

The video that was taken down when it had less than 50,000 views now reportedly has [five million views,](https://x.com/sabeer/status/2109484914609692893?s=20) as claimed by Bhatia himself. This only proves the Streisand effect, a psychological and social phenomenon where an attempt to hide, remove, or censor a piece of information inadvertently results in the widespread duplication and distribution of that specific or related information.
