





**# Andhra Pradesh clears data center projects worth Rs 2.83 lakh crore

Access combined version of four government orders here: [PDF]

Andhra Pradesh’s (AP) Information Technology, Electronics and Communications (ITE&C) Department has approved four AI data center projects and passed these three government orders (GOs) in August and one was amended in July. Here are the key details from the four GOs on AI data centers: 

Combined figures of four planned AI data centers in AP: 

- ~1,488 acres  (Excluding AM AI Factory)
    
- Rs.  2,83,340 crore proposed investment
    
- 4,050+ jobs (Excluding Google-Reliance AI data center)
    
- ~3,498 MW of power capacity
    

For some context of the scale, 

- 23 Narendra Modi Stadiums in Ahmedabad would fit in ~1,488 acres. It is the [largest](https://gujaratcricketassociation.com/narendra-modi-stadium/) stadium in the world by capacity.
    
- Proposed investments would cover over ~85% of AP’s Total Annual State Budget, which is [Rs. 3,32,205 cror](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/andhra-pradesh/andhra-pradesh-presents-332-lakh-crore-budget-for-202627-prioritises-education-welfare-and-fiscal-reforms/article70631533.ece)es for this financial year. 
    
- The planned 3,498 MW AI data center capacity is nearly 23.3% of the state's absolute peak load. During one of the extreme summer heatwaves, AP’s peak electricity demand reached an all-time record of [15,016 MW](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/summer-heat-ap-sees-highest-demand-for-power-at-15016mw-1958287) per that day’s 24 hours. 
    

All four were approved under the [AP’s Data Center Policy 4.0 (2024–29)](https://apiic.in/wp-content/themes/custom-theme/assets/Pdfs/2024ITC_33902_MS6.pdf), However, only Two of the four data center projects approved by Andhra Pradesh are explicitly stated as AI-focused: 

- Google-Reliance Industries' giga-scale AI data center in Vizianagaram and 
    
- AM AI Factory (Vizag) Pvt Ltd's 512 MW powered integrated AI data center in Amanam village, Visakhapatnam. 
    

The other two are classified as general data centers, not AI-specific. Here is the breakdown of the each AI data center’s details: 

1. Renew AP Green Data Center, Anakapalli: As part of the state incentives, this project will receive incentives worth up to Rs.14,400 crore, including stamp duty exemptions, capital subsidies, State GST refunds, and power discounts. 
    

Key details according to the government order: 

- Area: 433.30 acres, 
    
- Location: Makavaripalem, Anakapalli district
    
- Capacity: 1,000 MW
    
- Proposed Investment: INR 85,200 crore
    
- Proposed Job Creation: 1,880
    
- Estimated Timeline: 9 years, 5 month  
      
    

2. Data Centre Holdings India LLP, Visakhapatnam: AP government’s incentives include a 25% land discount, along with concessions for power, water, and for renewable energy. 
    

Key details according to the government order: 

- Area: 200 acres
    
- Location: Jagnadhapuram village, Anandapuram mandal, Visakhapatnam district
    
- Capacity: 486 MW
    
- Proposed Investment: INR 58,743 crore
    
- Proposed Job Creation: 1,250
    
- Estimated Timeline: 10 years
    

  

3. AM AI Factory (Vizag), Visakhapatnam: AM AI Factory (Vizag) Pvt Ltd is a new entity under AM Intelligence Labs, which holds over 51% stake. From the GO, it is not clear on who owns the remaining 49% of the stakes. As part of the state incentives, this project has 20-year exemptions on transmission, electricity duty, and cross-subsidy surcharges. 
    

Key details according to the government order: 

- Area: Not specified
    
- Location: Amanam village, Bheemunipatnam mandal, Visakhapatnam district
    
- Capacity: 512 MW (green integrated AI)
    
- Proposed Investment: INR 31,387 crore
    
- Proposed Job Creation: 1,000
    
- Estimated Timeline: 2 years, 4 months
    

  

4. Reliance Industries (Vizianagaram): In July 2026, Reliance Industries received an amendment for a giga-scale AI data centre in AP's Vizianagaram district. 
    

Key details according to the government order: 

- Area: up to 854.97 acres for the data center and 1 acre for a cable landing station
    
- Location: Vizianagaram district. 
    
- Capacity: Giga-scale AI data center
    
- Proposed Investment: Rs. 1,08,010 crore
    
- Proposed Job Creation: Not specified
    
- Estimated Timeline: Not specified
