**# RTI Filed: Where are Waves OTT’s monthly grievance reports required under IT Rules

We could not find the monthly grievance compliance reports required under the IT Rules for Prasar Bharati’s Waves OTT on its website. The [Terms and Conditions page](https://www.wavespb.com/terms-conditions) ([archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20260814112931/https://www.wavespb.com/terms-conditions)) only listed the details of the grievance officer but did not include any compliance reports.

So, we filed a few RTIs to seek more information. You can access the full questions below.

Why we are filing these RTIs:  OTT platforms must publish monthly grievance compliance reports under Rule 18(3) of the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. Major platforms operating in India, such as Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Jio Hotstar, ETV Win, SonlyLiv, and Aha Video, already do this. However, no public record exists for the government-owned Waves OTT platform.

Since Rule 18(3) applies to all publishers of online curated content, and government-owned platforms must meet the highest standards of public accountability, the absence of Waves OTT’s reports violates both the IT Rules, 2021, and the proactive public disclosure obligations under the RTI Act, 2005 too. 

Therefore, Prasar Bharati should publish all of its  monthly grievance compliance reports, if it is maintaining a record at all. And, if Prasar Bharati is not maintaining a record, then it should give an explanation for non-publication of monthly grievance compliance reports as obligated under the IT Rules. 

We asked the following questions to Ministry of Information and Broadcasting and Prasar Bharathi: 

To Prasar Bharathi: 

1. Please provide copies of the monthly grievance redressal or compliance reports submitted or required to be submitted for Waves OTT under Rule 18(3) of the IT Rules, 2021, from the date this obligation became applicable to Waves until the date of this RTI application. You may please redact names, contact details, or other identifying information of complainants if these reports contain personal data.
    
2. Please provide copies of any circulars, guidelines, office memoranda, emails, or letters issued by the Ministry to Prasar Bharati regarding its obligations under the IT Rules, 2021, specifically concerning the publication of monthly grievance reports on its website.
    
3. Please provide copies of any correspondence, show-cause notices, replies, or action reports related to non-submission, delayed submission, or non-publication of monthly grievance or compliance reports by Prasar Bharati or Waves OTT.
    

To Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, we have sent the same set of questions to MIB as well, along with an additional question: 

4. Please provide a list of OTT platforms that the Ministry has identified as being in compliance with the requirement to publish monthly grievance / compliance reports under Rule 18(3) of the IT Rules, 2021, along with copies of any records, dashboards, registers, compilations or periodic reviews through which the Ministry tracks and documents such compliance.
    

We will publish a fresh copy when Prasar Bharati or MIB responds. Meanwhile, if you want us to file a RTI, please [fill this form.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScjPo3qfbjYiei7v9gZ7M_5bBm_yppyh8DmccuE07atbAZMnQ/viewform) We'll file it on your behalf.

# RTI to PB 

  

Rule 18(3) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 requires OTT platforms publishing online curated content to release monthly compliance and grievance redressal reports. Major private platforms like Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, JioCinema, and SonyLIV already do this publicly. 

  

However, I could not find similar monthly grievance reports for Prasar Bharati’s OTT platform, Waves, on its website ([https://www.wavespb.com](https://www.wavespb.com)) or its terms and conditions page ([https://www.wavespb.com/terms-conditions](https://www.wavespb.com/terms-conditions)). The requested information pertains to statutory compliance and public accountability and does not involve commercially confidential details. 

  

Questions: 

(1).  Please provide copies of the monthly grievance redressal or compliance reports submitted or required to be submitted for Waves OTT under Rule 18(3) of the IT Rules, 2021, from the date this obligation became applicable to Waves until the date of this RTI application. You may please redact names, contact details, or other identifying information of complainants if these reports contain personal data.

(2). Please provide copies of any circulars, guidelines, office memoranda, emails, or letters issued by the Ministry to Prasar Bharati regarding its obligations under the IT Rules, 2021, specifically concerning the publication of monthly grievance reports on its website.

(3). Please provide copies of any correspondence, show-cause notices, replies, or action reports related to non-submission, delayed submission, or non-publication of monthly grievance or compliance reports by Prasar Bharati or Waves OTT.

If the information requested is held by another public authority, kindly transfer this application or the relevant part to the concerned authority under Section 6(3) of the RTI Act and inform me.
