# SC Seeks RBI, NPCI Replies on Whether MDR on UPI Is a Tax or Fee

The Supreme Court of India sought responses of Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and National Payments Council of India (NPCI) in a petition filed against Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) levied on UPI transactions above Rs. 2,000. "Is it tax or a fee? If not a fee, what is the executive basis for making this expropriation? What is the service?" Justice Joymlya Bagchi sought to know, according to the [LiveLaw’s](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-asks-centre-to-explain-decision-to-levy-charges-on-upi-transactions-refuses-stay-552055) report.

Reiterating Finance Minister Nirmala Seetharaman, Additional Solicitor General N Venkataraman stated that MDR on UPI is neither a tax or a fee or "[expropriation](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/expropriation) (government taking control over a private property)." He said that MDR on UPI is a cost sharing agreement among the involved parties like payment aggregators, banks and service providers. . 

Responding to this Chief Justice Surya Kant said, "We need these facts on affidavit. It's more of a technical issue."

MDR is being charged by the NPCI, the aggregator, the service provider, says FM Nirmala Seetharaman:  "This [MDR on UPI] is not a tax. This is not a cess. This is not even a surcharge. And the collection is not coming to the consolidated fund of India. So, let's first understand this is not a government issue. The MDR is being charged by the NPCI, the aggregator, the service provider, those who give the POS machines, the merchant bank. They are the ones who are providing the service. Above 2,000 rupees, the merchant is what is is who who's going to pay. And that money is not coming to Government of India and we are not imposing it," she said in an interview with [Press Trust of India.](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dAvjkWtiSIk)

Recently, NPCI's Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, Dilip Asbe, [stated](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-npci-ai-cyber-threats-mythos-upi-mdr/) that the risks of AI-enabled cyber attacks and the need to strengthening of UPI infrastructre as a defense for levying MDR on UPI. He was speaking at 13th State Bank of India (SBI) Banking & Economics Conclave on September 24.

"The 20 lakh server what we used to buy till last year, right now the same server costs about 1 crore. The costs have gone 5x. From NPCI perspective, in fact, we have almost utilised our IT budgets of this year and just got one-fourth or one-fifth of the material which we had already planned for.” -- NPCI's MD  

As the Supreme Court of India examines the nature of MDR on UPI, MediaNama [reported](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-govt-role-upi-mdr-gst/) he varied MDR on different sectors and their efforts to secure a lower MDR resembles the GST council and how various sectors sought for lesser GST rates. 

For instance, According to a [FAQ document published by NPCI](https://financialservices.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/FAQs---Merchant-Discount-Rate--MDR--on-Select-UPI--P2M--Transactions_0.pdf) after the MDR on UPI was [notified](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-upi-mdr-rs-2000-threshold/), 

- Railways, telecom, insurance, fuel and others have to pay a flat Rs 5 per payment above Rs 2,000
    
- Mutual funds and stockbrokers are charged 0.02%, capped at Rs 300
    
- Merchants (P2M) with more than one lakh income per month will be charged MDR
