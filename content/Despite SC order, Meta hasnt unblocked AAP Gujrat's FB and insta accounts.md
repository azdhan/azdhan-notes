**# Despite SC order, why Meta hasn’t restored Aam Aadmi Party’s regional accounts?  

Despite the Supreme Court of India's August 17 order to restore Gujrat's regional accounts of Aam Aadmi Party, Meta hasn't yet restored Instagram and Facebook accounts specified in the SC order.

"In the facts and circumstances of the case, pending disposal of the writ petition and as an interim measure, we deem it appropriate to direct the respondents to restore the Petitioner’s Instagram Handle “@aapgujarat” and the Facebook Page “@aapgujarat” subject to removal of objectionable material." -- Supreme Court of India. 

These are the concerned URLs:

- Facebook: [https://www.facebook.com/AAPGujarat/](https://www.facebook.com/AAPGujarat/)
    
- Instagram: [https://www.instagram.com/aapgujarat/](https://www.instagram.com/aapgujarat/)
    

What is the current status of these URLs:

- Instagram says the page is unavailable; couldn't access with a VPN: "Sorry, this page isn't available. The link you followed may be broken, or the page may have been removed. [Go back to Instagram.](https://www.instagram.com/)," reads the notice.
    
- Facebook restricted account in India and remains accessible via VPN: "Account not available in India. This is because we complied with a legal request to restrict this content," reads the notice.
    

‘If takedowns are instant, why isn't restoration?’ Speaking to MediaNama, a legal expert who deoesn’t want to be named said that since there aren’t codified rules for account/content restoration, he said, it is assumed that platforms should comply with the court orders as soon as possible. “After all, they takedown and block content ASAP. Then, why can’t they restore it?” he asked? 

Meta shows two different notices for same inaccessible AAP’s another at different times: Apart from these accounts, as noted in the supreme court's order, MediaNama found AAP's another Instagram account ([@aapjamnaaar](https://www.instagram.com/aapjamnaaar/)), which is currently inaccessible. 

Interestingly, Meta is displaying two different notices at two different time periods. This is what we discovered: 

- On Aug 17, 2026 at 5:17 pm, the notice read: "Account not available in India." 
    
- Whereas, at the time of writing this report, the notice reads: "Sorry, this page isn't available."
    

We couldn't understand this inconsistency. So, we sent a few questions to Meta. We'll update the copy if they respond to us.

Based on the [LiveLaw's reporting of the case proceedings](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-directs-meta-to-restore-aap-gujarats-facebook-instagram-pages-546088), here are a few key details to the case:

AAP's account has been suspended for 117 days: AAP's Gujarat Instagram and Facebook pages were blocked on April 24, 2026, ahead of local body elections in the state.

The suspension was linked to alleged copyright violations: AAP had allegedly used scenes and clips from Gujarati films in its campaign material without permission. AAP members claimed the action was politically motivated and that Meta blocked the accounts at the BJP's request. Currently, a case is being heard in Delhi HC examining how Meta’s copyright tools are being used for extortion as well as censorship, Read more about it [here](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-metas-copyright-delhi-hc-examine/). 

AAP's petition seeks following reliefs:

1. A declaration that Section 79(3)(b) of the IT Act does not authorize authorities to order the blocking of information.
    
2. A declaration that all rules, directions, and notifications issued under this provision for blocking information are invalid.
    
3. The cancellation of directions allegedly sent by law enforcement agencies to Meta to block or suspend these accounts.
    
4. A directive to the Court to request records of the blocking orders.
    
5. A declaration that blocking "@aapgujarat" is arbitrary, illegal, and unconstitutional.
    
6. Rules and safeguards for blocking or suspending official social media pages of registered political parties, ensuring such actions only occur after prior notice, an opportunity to be heard, and a written reason, and only on grounds permitted under Article 19(2) of the Constitution read with Section 69A of the IT Act.
    

MediaNama take: 

First, let's look at the compliance time frames for various purposes under the IT Rules. 

- 2 hours: Intimate or impersonation-related content must be taken down after a complaint.
    
- 3 hours: Content should be taken down if courts or government authorities order content removal.
    
- 24 hours: The Grievance Officer must acknowledge user complaints.
    
- 36 hours: Most Rule 3(1)(b) violations must be resolved. This rules lists the 11 categories of content users are not allowed to post or share on a platform.
    
- 72 hours: Requests for information or assistance from government agencies must be fulfilled.
    

Now, it's been over 117 days since AAP’s two social media accounts were blocked and still remains blocked after the over 48 hours of Supreme Court’s order. While there aren’t codified timeframes for unblocking/restoring accounts/content, how should we interpret the delayed restoration? Moreover, what would happen if the platform doesn’t comply at all? What punietive actions should the contempt of court invite if it's applicable?

Additonally, it is important to note that MeitY summoned Meta’s executives after the so-called ‘accidental’ geo-blocking of PM Modi’s video reel for over five hours. Meta was asked to apologize for ther “error” and was served with the threats of safe harbour. If Meta should be held accountable for restricting the highest public office holder PM Modi’s reel, then shouldn’t the same principle apply to public officials of lesser cadre then PMO and common citizens too? What is the rationale for special treatment to the individuals/regime in power?

Moreoever, if Meta should be held accountable/liable for its actions, should Meta be held accountable for disabling access to PM Modi’s short-video address temporarily? Or should it be held accountable for its arbitrary censorship and mindless compliance with the government takedown/blocking requests/orders?
