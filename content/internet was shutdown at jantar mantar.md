## Internet Shutdown at Jantar Mantar Projects after Delhi Police breaks Sonam Wangchuk's indefinite huger strike

Mobile internet services were disrupted in parts of central Delhi as large crowds gathered at Jantar Mantar for a protest, according to several posts on X. 

MediaNama's reporter Prabhanu Kumar Das confirmed the internet shutdown through the individuals who were present at the protest site. 

**Aroon Deep** ([@AroonDeep](https://x.com/AroonDeep)), posting from the location, [said](https://x.com/AroonDeep/status/2079072729232486875?s=20) mobile internet is shut down at the protest site. Another user [posted](https://x.com/MrPanndey/status/2079067120470745452?s=20) "Internet is not working at the jantar mantar and surrounding areas." In a follow-up post, Deep [said](https://x.com/AroonDeep/status/2079072843040764150?s=20) he was only able to send updates through a nearby Jio Wi-Fi hotspot with a limited 34MB of data. Read The Hindu's brief post on X based on Deep's reporting [here](https://x.com/the_hindu/status/2079078155994968572?s=20). 

**CJP_for_India** ([@CJP_for_India](https://x.com/CJP_for_India)) posted that some mobile users in the Jantar Mantar area had received a message about the internet shutdown, adding, The account said the message read: "As per government instructions, internet services had been stopped in the area until further notice. REGINF."

A [screenshot](https://x.com/amock202/status/2079083633655357590?s=20) posted by **@amock202**, showing a notification from an Airtel sender ID, similarly read that "the internet services have been temporarily stopped in your area," per instructions attributed to the government, with the message repeated in the notification.

The Wire [reported](https://x.com/thewire_in/status/2079086722865070196?s=20) that signal jammers had disrupted mobile networks at Jantar Mantar, forcing many to walk nearly 2 km away from the protest site to access the internet or to make calls. 

**A brief context:** Climate activist and educator Sonam Wangchuk was [forcefully removed ](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/18/world/asia/sonam-wangchuk-hunger-strike-delhi-police.html)from Delhi's Jantar Mantar by the Delhi Police on Day 21 of his indefinite hunger strike and taken to Safdarjung Hospital. The action followed a [Delhi High Court order](https://delhihighcourt.nic.in/app/showlogo/1784189116_0a7a8c304d45dcf9_683_94982026.pdf/2026) to monitor his health and it later [refused](https://lawbeat.in/amp/news-updates/sonam-wangchuk-protest-delhi-hc-refuses-urgent-hearing-on-pil-challenging-police-action-1613877) to listen to a Public Interest Litigation filed against illegal detention of the activist.
