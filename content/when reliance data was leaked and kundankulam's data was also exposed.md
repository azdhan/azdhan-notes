**# Reliance and Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant data breach; what we found on darkweb 

MediaNama was able to access Reliance's leaked data on dark web, which also included data related to Tamil Nadu’s Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant. The leaked data is publicly available to anyone who has access to the onion link of the ransom group’s website. We are not disclosing the onion link to preserve the confidentiality of the leaked information. 

  

Independent cybersecurity researcher Rakesh Krishnan.flagged Reliance’s data leakage on World Leaks to Reuters and reportedly said that the 19,000 files (14.3 GB) data has been available since June 11. You can read the Reuter’s original reporting [here](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/files-relating-indias-largest-nuclear-power-plant-kudankulam-exposed-data-breach-2026-07-15/). 

  

Reliance confirmed a "partial breach" of data on a server hosted by third-party provider Mumbai-based Yotta, according to Reuters. However, the company did not disclose what data was breached. According to the unnamed sources quoted in the Reuters report, Nuclear Power Corporation of India (NPCIL) has been in contact with Reliance regarding the data breach and apparently CERT-In is looking into the incident.

  

What Reliance has to do with Kudankulam Nuclear Powerplant? In 2018, Reliance group won over Rs, 1,081 Crore contractual project to third and fourth units of the nuclear plant. You can read the press release [here, on Reliance Infra’s website.](https://www.rinfra.com/documents/1142822/10625710/Letterto_SE_Media_Release_09042018.pdf)

  

What we found inside the leaked database? According to the database available on World Leak’s onion websites, the size of the data is 1.2 TB and consists of over 858,253 files which was last updated on June 13, two days after cybersecrity researcher Krishnan first spotted the leak. Because of human constraints and poor server strength, we couldn’t examine allt he content that is available on the database. Since this is allegedly leaked data, we couldn’t confirm the authenticity of the files.   

  

Some of the information we found from the leaked data:

- Finance and payroll data, including income tax data (2018-19) and invoices
    
- Bank statements of individuals 
    
- Tender documents inside KKNNP Project folder
    
- A folder named “KKNPP Impotent Letter” with PDFs 
    
- Human Resource (HR), including full resumes of individuals.
    
- Technical inspection reports
    
- Chemical equipments
    
- Cables and its maintenance data
