Now we will have a network that will learn on its own. I did not teach the network. This is not the first time Jio has publicly acknowledged the operations of network slicing. When the Department of Telecommunications has sent a letter to TRAI seeking the recommendations on net neutrality concerns around network slicing, MediaNama [reported](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-dot-trai-net-neutrality-review-jio/) that Jio already had network slicing deployed.

 “We are the only ones who are running six commercial network slices in the production network today,” said Aayush Bhatnagar, President of Reliance Jio.

Here are the recent regulatory developments concerning net neutrality and network slicing: 

- TRAI: In August, TRAI [released](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-trai-5g-network-slicing-quality-of-service/) the Quality of Service framework for the 5G networks that could allow formalize network slicing in India. 
    
- Jio: As part of these consulttions, , Reliance Jio and GSMA [urged](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-jio-gsma-network-slicing-net-neutrality/) TRAI to allow service-based network slicing as the regulator considers new quality-of-service rules.  
    
- MediaNama: Upholding the principles of net neutrality, MediaNama [submitted](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-network-slicing-medianama-trai/) counter comments to TRAI, arguing that net neutrality regulations should govern network slices as well.
    
- BIF: Later, Broadband India Forum [warned](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-bif-application-network-slicing-net-neutrality/) that application-specific network slicing could breach net neutrality, saying that giving different apps different speeds would lead to content discrimination. However, during the initial stakeholder comments submissions, COAI and BIF [did not make](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-coai-bif-submissions-trai-network-slicing/) stakeholder submissions
    
- Jio: Arguing against the provision that proposes telcos to maintain network capacity, Jio [opposed](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-jio-opposes-trais-network-slicing/) TRAI's proposed 80% network utilisation threshold for 5G network slicing, urging the regulator to withdraw the restrictions and saying high utilisation does not necessarily mean congestion or slower speeds.
    
- TRAI open house: At TRAI's open house discussion, MediaNama [shared](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-medianama-comments-trai-ohd-network-slicing-net-neutrality/) its views on network slicing, net neutrality and telecom operators.
    
- TRAI open house: At the same open house discussion, BIF, consumer groups, Airtel and Jio [shared](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-trai-network-slicing-net-neutrality-stakeholders/) their views on TRAI's proposed framework for 5G network slicing and quality of service.
    

Addressing the AI-driven decision making and human decision making in the network slicing decisions. During the same IMC discussion, Vodafone's Praveen Sharma EVP, Technology Projects said particular network slicing is a commercial and strategic monetization decision. Because slicing involves carving out dedicated network capabilities that directly impact revenue, SLAs, and business strategy, he argued it is a high-stakes action that must remain under explicit human control, not delegated to an autonomous AI agent.

"There are certain actions in operators like regulatory actions, lawful interception, monetization of the network like we want to monetise the particular frequency, particular slicing. So those actions should be left to humans only." - Praveen Sharma, Vodafone Idea. 

What is a RAN system and how will it interact with Network Slicing: The Radio Access Network (RAN) is the part of a mobile network that connects your phone to the rest of the networks, consists of antennas and the radio equipment that sends and receives signals over the air. Everything a user does on a mobile network starts with the RAN. 

RAN slicing typically works by [distributing and allocating these radio resources among slices](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.08244)  Vendors now offer products that do this dynamically. Ericsson, for instance, says its RAN slicing product [allocates resources on a one-millisecond basis](https://www.lightreading.com/5g/ericsson-looks-to-ran-slicing-for-5g-boost/d/d-id/766899), which it says improves the end-user experience and spectral efficiency.
