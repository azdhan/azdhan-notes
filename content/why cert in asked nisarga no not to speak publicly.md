# Explained: Why did CERT-In ask cybersecurity researcher Nisarga to stop publicly disclosing vulnerabilities?

What happened? On August 28, CERT-In [wrote](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2093416181743137056/photo/1) to cybersecurity researcher Nisarga Adhikary asking stop public disclosures of the vulnerabilities he finds while the "coordination/remediation" of the vulnerabilities is still underway.

Replying to CERT-In, Adhikary refused to stop speaking about the vulnerabilities, especially if the concerned organizations don't respond to the disclosed vulnerability within a general timeframe like 3-4 weeks. 

"i do this research independently, for free, to help secure systems in this country nobody pays me to find, document, report, chase and retest these vulnerabilities. there are far more lucrative and far less responsible ways valuable exploits could be handled. i CHOOSE to report them responsibly. you are the one who is getting PAID here, not me. this is NOT a bug bounty program where you can order me to follow your pointless rules. and THIS is cert-in's response? a lecture about my tweets?" -- Nisarga Adhikary in a [reply](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2093416181743137056/photo/1) to CERT-In. 

What is the researcher's defence? He said that he gives rganisations 3-4 weeks to fix vulnerabilities and he doesn’t publish anything that is actionable, like Proof of Concepts or reproduction steps, or endpoints, or credentials.  “STOP pretending that saying ‘critical vulnerabilities remain unfixed’ is the same as publishing an exploit.”

He also called out the 'irresponsibility, incompetency of CERT-in and the audacity to police his tweets. 

Some background: In May 2026, Nisarga Adhikary first made the headlines when he exposed the key vulnerabilities in the CBSE’s On-Screen Marking portal to CERT-In. He first disclosed the vulnerabilities to CERT-In. He went public only when [CERT-In hadn't responded](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-cert-in-vulnerabilities-cbse-online-marking-portal/) or fixed the vulnerabilities. Later, after the [denials from the officials](https://x.com/cbseindia29/status/2059305761713574122?s=20), he [demonstrated the access](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-cbse-hacked-cybersecurity-researcher-demonstrates-access-official-denials/) to their backend system. He is now currently working as OSINT & Threat Intelligence Engineer at C3iHub, IIT Kanpur. IIT Kanpur [offered](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/teen-who-flagged-cbse-flaws-lands-full-time-job-at-iit-kanpur/articleshow/131668942.cms) him the full-time job after the CBSE fiasco. 

Flags vulnerability that enables him to send emails from @gov.in domain name: He also flagged another incident with CERT-In. In his email to CERT-In, he [stated](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2094422267078090909?s=20) that he found a vulnerability that lets him send an email from the @gov.in domain name. In fact, he sent the email to CERT-In from the @gov.in domain. 

https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2094422267078090909?s=20 

CERT-In’s cybersecurity experts don't kow curl commands, says Adhikary: After this incident of CERT-In's attempts to silence the researcher, he [shared](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2093735082154655906/photo/1) the screenshots from May 2026 to indicate the incompetency of CERT-In. After he disclosed the vulnerabilities to CERT-In, CERT-In responded asking for the "step-by-step Proof of Concept (PoC)" despite the researcher sharing the commands as the PoC. 

"Gentle reminder that the “scientists” at CERT-In do not know how to run curl commands," he later wrote on X. curl commands [refers](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/linux-unix/curl-command-in-linux-with-examples/) to the command-line-based communications with websites without relying on typical web browsers. 

Some more comments on CERT-in by Nisarga: 

- "there’s genuinely no point reporting to CERT-In. they’ll take credit for your research, fail to get the vulnerabilities fixed, never even mention your name in the hall of fame, and then waste hours of your time chasing “remediation” that never happened," he [said](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2094351976096231661?s=20) in a reply 
    
- "Just figured out CERT-In has not been able to confirm the remediation of more than 1% of the vulnerabilities I have reported in the past few months. Painful," he [posted](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2093641451439796648?s=20) on August 29.
    
- “Reported critical vulns in police/law enforcement infra to cert-in a month ago. still unpatched. cert-in keeps wasting my time with “patched, please retest” mails, and every single retest proves the same exploits still work. absolute shitshow,” he [posted](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2092548730486653312?s=20) on August 26. 
    
- “credit stealing final boss. cert-in didn’t “detect” shit. i sent them my initial cbse security report in february, then 3+ more reports in may covering other vulnerabilities in the osm portal. somehow my research became their “detection.” incredible work guys.” he [wrote](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2093618971522326877) on August 29.
    
- "Reported vulnerabilities affecting a major Indian bank to CERT-In almost two weeks ago, yet they still haven’t been patched. Sent another report regarding a different Indian bank last night. What is even happening?" he [wrote](https://x.com/ni5arga/status/2082996891906949144) on July 31.
