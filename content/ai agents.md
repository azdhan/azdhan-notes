there are two types of agentic behaviour and the approaches: 
- **Agent Fleet:** A collection of **independent agents working in parallel on the same task without communicating** with one another. Each agent operates in isolation to complete its piece of the work.

- **Swarm:** A collective group of agents that **interact, communicate, and coordinate** with each other (often mimicking biological swarms like ants or bees) to achieve a joint goal through collective behavior
