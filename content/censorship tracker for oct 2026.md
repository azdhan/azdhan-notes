# mistral Live Tracker: Censorship and Digital Developments in India

*Last updated: October 29, 7.54 am*

Instagram, Facebook and X have [withheld posts, videos and accounts in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115273539792363844) since at least October 21, citing legal requests under Section 79(3)(b) and Section 69A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, Rule 4(4) of the IT Rules, 2021, and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026. The takedowns, documented by the tracker [@geoblockedindia](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115300770279039305), cover political commentary, satire, finance content, wildlife posts and videos [explaining constitutional articles](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115275221783728431).

Restrictions reported since October 26 include content about protests against alleged [vote chori](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115375645496840611), a ["Chalo Dilli" rally](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115296377165271235) planned for November 1, [Arunachal Pradesh and India–China tensions](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115399180474887080), and the [Adani US fraud case](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115365868289098096).

Account-level actions include the blocking of the news page [@4pmnews_network](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115273539792363844), restrictions on the accounts of Khurpenchh and Dr Mohammad Momin, and a [Section 94 BNSS notice](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115473714049368243) to Khurpenchh from the Patna Cyber Police.

In this context, MediaNama is documenting online censorship and other digital developments in India through this live tracker.

### Censorship developments:

**Patna Cyber Police sent Khurpenchh a Section 94 BNSS notice:** Khurpenchh (@khurpenchh) [said](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115262584534519986) their Facebook account had been banned for three months, Instagram had been down for 72 hours, and they had received a legal notice for content removal from the Cyber Crime and Security Unit, Bihar Police. *UPDATE*: Khurpenchh, whose Instagram and Facebook accounts were blocked in India, [received](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115473714049368243) a Section 94 BNSS notice from the Patna Cyber Police seeking account details and removal of a video. Khurpenchh said they will submit an affidavit soon but will not take down the video, according to the tracker [@geoblockedindia](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115473714049368243).

**Advocate sent Meta a ₹1 crore notice over geo-blocked Reels:** Advocate Meet R. Trivedi [said](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115448866187407528) 8 of his Instagram Reels were geo-blocked in India, and his Facebook Page, Instagram and Threads accounts were temporarily banned. He has sent Meta a ₹1 crore legal notice demanding disclosure of the underlying orders and compensation, the tracker reported.

**Instagram blocked the 4pmnews_network account in India:** The Instagram account of @4pmnews_network, with 880,000 followers, was [blocked in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115273539792363844) under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026, the tracker reported.

**MeitY is restricting content by keywords, tracker reported:** MeitY is reportedly using keywords to restrict Instagram and Facebook videos and accounts, including educational videos explaining constitutional articles, without assessing the content individually, the tracker [reported](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115274818329464982). A video by @amitkilhor explaining a constitutional article was [reportedly blocked](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115275221783728431) in India under the IT Amendment Rules, 2026.

**Meta removed comments and posts based on keywords:** Meta is [removing comments](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115285600568000633) based on certain keywords, according to several users who shared examples of comments being taken down. Multiple users reported restrictions on Instagram posts and Stories involving keywords related to Modi, Rahul Gandhi, protests, vote chori, the BJP, the Congress and the Election Commission, sometimes within minutes of posting, the tracker [reported](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115399077030773121), citing @BhupenderG50804.

**X withheld a video on threats against Muslims by Bajrang Dal:** A video by @mehanged documenting threats against Muslims by Bajrang Dal members was [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115630828747387075) on October 21 under Section 79(3)(b) of the Information Technology Act, 2000, following a government or law-enforcement notice, the tracker reported on October 29.

**X withheld a post by Veena under Section 69A:** An X post by @veenapa63862707 was [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115389907770462455) under Section 69A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, following a blocking order from MeitY, the tracker reported.

**X withheld a Satish Mishra video about Modi:** An X video by Satish Mishra (@SatishM93279435) captioned "मोगैंबो मोदी कब खुश होगा" [Mogambo, when will Modi be pleased] was [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115627661951418756) on October 28 under Rule 4(4) of the IT Rules, 2021, pursuant to a legal request, the tracker reported.

**Instagram withheld photos of Rahul Gandhi meetings in India:** Instagram photos featuring Rahul Gandhi, including posts by the NSUI and photos captioned "With boss ❤️" and describing a meeting with Rahul Gandhi in Delhi, were [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115627073217913329) on October 28 under "local legal requirements", according to the tracker, which documented the takedowns.

**Instagram withheld vote chori protest content in India:** An Instagram Reel by @kaunrahulcom featuring Rahul Gandhi during a protest against alleged vote chori was [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115375645496840611) on October 28 under the IT Amendment Rules, 2026, the tracker reported. An Instagram post by @thegrumpyindian captioned "Vote Chori" was [blocked](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115398924567752729) in India following a legal request.

**Instagram restricted Chalo Dilli rally posts in India:** An Instagram post calling for attendance at a "Chalo Dilli" opposition rally scheduled for November 1 was [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115268400327053698), with the restriction citing legal requirements. Several other posts related to the same November 1 event, including one calling against vote chori, were also [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115296377165271235), according to the tracker.

**Instagram withheld Arunachal Pradesh and China tension content:** Instagram posts, Stories and Reels related to Arunachal Pradesh and India–China tensions have been increasingly [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115399180474887080), with numerous reports received since October 26. The account @RedDeFacto was [blocked in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115394346623414781) over content related to Arunachal Pradesh–China tensions, reportedly for spreading fake news.

**Instagram withheld Adani-related posts in India:** An Instagram post by Swanaam Dhaanya (@swanaamdhaanya) about the Adani US fraud case was [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115365868289098096) under the IT Amendment Rules, 2026, the tracker reported. An Instagram reel by Anubhav Gupta (@anubhavgupta_ji) related to Gautam Adani and Mukesh Ambani was also [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115271913836802459) under the same provision.

**Instagram withheld a post on Pakistan minister's SCO visit within minutes:** An Instagram post by Swanaam Dhaanya about the Modi government inviting Pakistan's minister to the SCO Cultural Forum was [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115391767734608176) within two minutes of posting on October 28, the tracker reported.

**Facebook made Ashok Dadhwal's posts unavailable in India:** Multiple Facebook posts, photos, Reels and Stories by Ashok Dadhwal (@ashokdadhwal196) were made [unavailable in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115370461769130154) under local legal requirements cited under India's IT rules, the tracker reported. One post concerned Rahul Gandhi's remarks on protests and alleged electoral irregularities.

**Instagram withheld two pro-Modi videos by Amit Kilhor:** Two Instagram videos by @amitkilhor, uploaded on August 21 and January 31, were [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115345245177352214) on October 27. Both contained pro-government, pro-Modi content and used the caption "MODIJI" or "Organizers please take note and rectify it.", the tracker reported.

**Instagram took down a RealMaxtern video on Bihar Chief Minister:** An Instagram video by @RealMaxtern discussing the Bihar Chief Minister was [taken down](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115263369213288776). The video was based on publicly available articles and factual information about the Bihar Chief Minister, according to the tracker.

**Instagram restricted a doctor's handle and a ragging video was deleted:** The Instagram handle of Dr Mohammad Momin (@DrMohammadMomin) was reportedly [restricted](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115368295549595848), after which a video about alleged ragging at GMC Jalaun, Uttar Pradesh, was automatically deleted, the tracker reported.

**Instagram withheld a cartoon citing IT Rules without naming an order:** A cartoon by @aaaaadvik, originally posted on September 29 with [@internetfreedom](https://x.com/internetfreedom/status/2104881564933062857) and Pulkit Mani, was [withheld in India](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115283665777561901) on October 27. Instagram cited the IT Rules, 2021, but named no specific order, authority or legal provision, the tracker reported.

**Instagram withheld political and finance posts under 2026 amendment rules:** Instagram [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115368954789351753) multiple posts by TradingCopter (@TradingCopter) featuring Indian finance content, including PM Modi and Nirmala Sitharaman, under the IT Amendment Rules, 2026, and [restricted](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115348366154744047) multiple posts by Pushpendra Singh (@pushpendrakum) under the same provision. A post by Fearless Amit (@Fearless_Amit) reading "मोदी कहीं नहीं जाएगा" [Modi will not go anywhere] was [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115346316503244868) on October 28. Two posts by Krishnadeesha (@Krishnadeesha), including one on Amir Khusro's life and cultural legacy, were [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115345841129243037) under the same provision. A post by DuttShekhar (@DuttShekhar) criticising political attacks between the Congress and the BJP was [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115337433583955997), as was a post by NarativeByVirat (@NarativeByVirat) [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115336256188219856) under the same provision.

**Instagram withheld wildlife, cricket and lifestyle posts under same rules:** Instagram [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115347191024021567) a post by Wildlife of Andhra Pradesh (@wildlife_ap) about an elephant's electrocution death in Chittoor under the IT Amendment Rules, 2026. Two cricket-related photos posted by @Tejashyy on October 25 were [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115393418671399169) under the same provision. One photo, three Stories and two reels by @kafkasbluebug were [withheld](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115264005732516314) between October 9 and October 26. Instagram also [removed](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115310867755459054) 7 posts by @More2Wish under the same provision.

**LiveLaw's previously withheld Instagram reel is available again:** An Instagram reel by LiveLaw that had previously been withheld in India is now [available again](https://x.com/geoblockedindia/status/2115264481723040249), the tracker reported on October 28.
