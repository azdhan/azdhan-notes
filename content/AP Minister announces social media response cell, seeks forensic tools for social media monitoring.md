

Andhra Pradesh's Minister for Information & Public Relations, Kolusu Parthasarathy, has announced that the state will form a "social media response cell" and a task force to "investigate allegations of unethical practices on social media." Watch his brief media interaction [here](https://x.com/kpsarathyTDP/status/2075601486277665021?s=20 ), on his X profile. 

**What are unethical practices according to the AP Minister?** 
- **Targeting/torturing girls and women** on social media, including harassment or abuse.
- **Spreading lies about the government**: Including, disinformation and/or defamation campaigns
- **Targeting politicians and trying to exploit or take advantage of them** though character assasination, blackmail, or abusive smear campaigns against public figures

**Advanced forensic tools for the social media monitoring, state sets aside budget:** He further said that government should hand over the responsibility to investigate and monitor these alleged unethical issues using the advanced forensic tools. He added that the state has dedicated budget for this purpose. 

**AP Minister's interpretation of unethical practices echos AP recent Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan's list** of things which are not considered considered part of free speech, which include: 
- Obscene language
- Baseless personal attacks
- Attempts to destroy reputations
- Fake propaganda
- Morphed images
- Death threats

Recent incidents of raising online censorship in Andhra: 
-  **AP police arrest YouTuber and journalist over content**: Andhra Pradesh police arrested YouTuber Raavan (B. Joseph) of the Prashna channel and journalist-YouTuber K. Venkatrami Reddy (KVR) of Myra Media, months after arresting a stand-up comedian. Reddy was arrested four times despite getting bail from various courts. - **Meta geo-blocks AP opposition party's Facebook page**: Meta made the official YSRCP Facebook page inaccessible in India, following the earlier takedown of the party's Instagram handle. Former AP CM YS Jagan Mohan Reddy said screenshots showed Meta complied with blocking orders from the Government of India/law enforcement. The page was later restored and made accessible without a VPN. [Read more about this issue from MediaNama archives](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-rising-political-censorship-ap-meta-blocked-ap-opposition-partys-facebook-page/)
- **Meta blocks Environmental Reporting Collective's Instagram reel on Google's AI data center**: Meta geo-restricted a two-minute documentary reel featuring farmers from Tarluvada village alleging forced land acquisition for a Google AI data center near Visakhapatnam. The Environmental Reporting Collective, a global journalist network, criticized the lack of transparency behind the takedown. [Read more about this issue from MediaNama archives](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-meta-blocks-environmental-reporting-collectives-instagram-reel-googles-ai-data-centers-ap/)
- **Meta restricts Human Rights Forum's Instagram posts on the same data center project**: A week after the Environmental Reporting Collective takedown, Meta also restricted two HRF Instagram posts; one seeking accountability on the data center project, another documenting construction near the Mudasarlova reservoir. HRF demanded restoration of the content and a clear, itemised legal explanation. Instead of showing a restriction notice, Instagram quietly redirected non-VPN users to unrelated algorithm-recommended reels. [Read more about this issue from MediaNama archives](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-meta-human-rights-forum-instagram-googles-ai-data-center-andhra/)
- **Bengaluru court orders block on content alleging land encroachment by AP DyCM Pawan Kalyan**: A Bengaluru Urban Court passed a John Doe gag order directing X, Google, and Meta to block all defamatory content about Pawan Kalyan until the next hearing on July 24, 2026. The plea cited videos and posts alleging encroachment of a water body in Ranga Reddy, Telangana, and claimed IT Rules grievance mechanisms had failed to resolve the issue. [Read more about this issue from MediaNama archives](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-bengaluru-court-orders-block-content-alleging-land-encroachment-ap-dycm-pawan-kalyan/)
- **AP police detain stand-up comedians over jokes on leaders**: Police detained comedian Anudeep Katikala over YouTube video jokes; after his release, comic Rafiq Mohammed was arrested for alleged jokes about DyCM Pawan Kalyan and IT Minister Nara Lokesh.
