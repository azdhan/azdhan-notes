
Karnataka CM announces 'green data centre' and AI university at Google I/O Connect India

*Explore Google I/O Connect India Event 2026: [YouTube Live](https://www.youtube.com/live/y4AHfhPTIW0?t=1773s) |  [Google Blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/company-news/google-io-connect-india-2026/)*

"We are also establishing a next-generation green data centre in a coastal district as well as near Bengaluru. That is a major initiative we are taking up," [said](https://www.youtube.com/live/y4AHfhPTIW0?t=1773s) Karnataka CM DK Shivakumar at Google's I/O Connect India 2026 event in Bengaluru. 

He also announced that Karnataka state government want to "lay the foundation for the first and largest AI university" for talent building. 

**A few key takeaways from the event are:** 

**How Delhi AIIMS is using Google's MedGemma Models:** "I'm thrilled to share that AIIMS is now also using MedGemma to build models for two more use cases: one for leprosy, and one for sexual and reproductive health. These new models will support patients, doctors and healthcare professionals in identifying and managing conditions based on images and text inputs. The outcomes of all the models that AIIMS Delhi is building will be open-sourced, to contribute to India's digital public infrastructure. The tools to solve incredibly complex, localised problems for India, and for every Indian, are in your hands right now. We cannot wait to see how you innovate with them," said Somesh Gupta, Professor of Dermatology at AIIMS Delhi. 

"The app takes an image and a text description of symptoms to help identify skin conditions. MedGemma brings together clinical images, patient history and symptom descriptions using multimodal AI to generate structured, context-aware clinical insight, helping healthcare professionals make faster and more informed decisions," he further added.

 "National Health Authority has leveraged our MedGemma models and open-source toolkit for its new Aarogya Setu 2.0 app. This is opening a new chapter for digital health in India," said Dr. Manish Gupta, Senior Director of Research, at Google DeepMind. He also said that "large enterprises like Tata Steel are using our AI on Google Cloud to strengthen customer relations, employee experience."

In June 2026, Union Health Minister JP Nadda launched Aarogya Setu 2.0, which can function as an AI-enabled Personal Health Record (PHR) management platform and a centralized application for digital health-related services in India. The proposed centralised health platform plans to use Google's Gemma 4. Read more about it [here](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-aarogya-setu-2-google-gemma-health-records/). 

**How Google plans to deploy AI in education:** "To further equip the educators preparing India's future leaders, Google DeepMind is deepening our collaboration with the Atal Innovation Mission. We are launching ATL Saathi, a desktop and web application that supports teachers with a Gemini-powered assistant. It helps teachers deliver the Atal Tinkering Labs curriculum through engaging video and audio overviews, and curates hands-on experiments for young tinkerers in the lab. We are rolling out ATL Saathi to 100 schools across the country, with the aim of reaching 10,000 schools by the end of the year," added Gupta. Read more about this [here, on Google's blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/empowering-indias-next-generation-of-innovators-with-atl-saathi/).
