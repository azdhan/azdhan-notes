## COAI and BIF haven’t made stakeholder submissions to TRAI on network slicing 

The Cellular Operators Association of India (COAI) and the Broadband India Forum (BIF) [haven't made submissions](https://www.trai.gov.in/consultation-paper-draft-amendments-standards-quality-service-access-wireline-and-wireless-and) to [TRAI’s stakeholder consultations on network slicing](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-trai-5g-network-slicing-quality-of-service/). Both organizations have been vocal on network slicing in the past but chose not to participate in the formal consultation process.

A few telecom companies have told MediaNama that there is a difference of opinion among COAI members on the issue of network slicing, and hence they couldn’t come to a consensus on this issue. MediaNama has emailed COAI seeking clarifications on why it hasn’t participated in consultation process. If they respond, we’ll update the copy with their response.

Why do their submissions matter: Their representations as industry bodies matter as COAI represents the following [telecom entities](https://coai.com/core-members) like Airtel, Jio and Vodafone Idea; [telecom infrastructure companies](https://coai.com/associate-members) like Ericsson, Nokia, Indus Towers, Qualcomm and tech giants like Google, Meta and Zoom.

Apart from the above-mentioned companies, which are also part of BIF, some of the companies part of BIF are Samsung, Microsoft, AWS, AT&T, Netflix, JioStar, Tata Communications, TCS, WhatsApp, OneWeb, and OpenAI. BIF also [represents](https://broadbandindiaforum.in/members/) a few public sector undertakings (PSUs) like BSNL, the Centre for Development of Telematics (C-DOT), ITI Limited, RailTel, and DoT-owned Telecommunications Consultants India Limited

Moreover, what BIF and COAI think regarding network slicing is more relevant, as they have made a few critical and important remarks regarding network slicing in the past. Interestingly, COAI held contradictory views on network slicing and net neutrality in 2022 and 2026. 

 2022 - Network slicing violates the net neutrality, said COAI: In 2022, SP Kochhar, Director General, COAI, said that the flexibility of 5G networks arises from network slicing and edge computing. Therefore, he said that it directly negates the principles of net neutrality at a panel discussion at the 5G India Leadership Summit 2022. "The moment you start doing the permutations and combinations of network slicing and edge computing, you are giving preferred services to a particular segment. Net neutrality does not permit that," he said, as quoted in the [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/telecom/telecom-policy/5g-network-slicing-may-lead-to-flouting-of-net-neutrality-rules-coais-kochhar/articleshow/91670116.cms) report. 

2026 - Network slicing does NOT violate net neutrality, said COAI: After [Airtel launched Priority Postpaid using network slicing](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-airtel-first-indian-telecom-operator-5g-network-slicing-services-postpaid/), Kochhar stated that 'slicing in general would not violate net neutrality.' In the [Economic Times](https://telecom.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/industry/reliance-jio-supports-bharti-airtels-5g-network-slicing-amid-net-neutrality-debate/131326240) report, he further stated that, ‘given pricing and service quality, network slicing would unlock the benefits of the next generation (5G) network.

COAI welcomes network slicing after Airtel’s ‘Fast Lane’: During the same time, after Airtel’s network slicing, now rebranded as [Fast Lane](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-airtel-first-indian-telecom-operator-5g-network-slicing-services-postpaid/), COAI released a statement welcoming network slicing. Read the statement published on [Tele.Net:](https://tele.net.in/coai-releases-statement-on-5g-network-slicing/) “We welcome the introduction of 5G network slicing capabilities in India, marking an important milestone in the evolution of next-generation digital infrastructure. Network slicing is a cutting-edge 5G technology that has already been launched in markets such as the United States, Singapore, China, and parts of Europe. We are delighted that one of our operators has now brought this innovation to India, reflecting the rapid progress of the country’s telecom ecosystem. It is equally encouraging to see India catching up with the world’s most advanced digital economies in adopting such transformative technologies. With continued innovation and investment, we look forward to India not just keeping pace, but emerging as a global leader in advanced 5G capabilities.” 

COAI calls for network slicing for enterprise 5G networks: On a proposal for direct allocation of spectrum to private networks, COAI called for service delivery for enterprise 5G networks via licensed telecom service providers (TSPs) instead of dedicated spectrum allocation for private networks. “COAI strongly believes that all enterprise 5G needs must be fulfilled through licensed Telecom Service Providers via spectrum leasing or network slicing as this would ensure national security, revenue protection and regulatory parity in this rapidly evolving ecosystem,” reported [Tele.Net](https://tele.net.in/coai-releases-a-statement-on-proposal-for-direct-allocation-of-spectrum-to-private-networks/) based on the COAI’s statement on this issue. 

In short, after initially arguing in 2022 that network slicing violated net neutrality, COAI shifted to mandating it via licensed telecom providers (TSPs) for enterprise 5G by 2025 and, by 2026, declared that network slicing is fully compliant with existing net neutrality rules. [This is the same view held by Jio too.](https://www.medianama.com/2026/03/223-telecom-push-network-slicing-net-neutrality-norms-relaxation/) 

What did BIF say about network slicing? 

Telcos shouldn’t treat consumers differently on sliced networks: "If two automotive companies, say Maruti and Toyota, seek similar services [through network slicing], they should not receive different treatment," [said](https://www.medianama.com/2026/03/223-telecom-push-network-slicing-net-neutrality-norms-relaxation/) TV Ramachandran, President, BIF, at the TRAI Day event in February 2026. He also said that “there is going to be a heavy onus on TRAI for monitoring network slicing.” “That is very important to protect the consumer's interest, not only the individual personal consumer, but also the business customer," he added. He further called for telcos to publish something similar to a [Reference Interconnect Offer (RIO](https://www.trai.gov.in/release-publication/regulations/amendments-page/7206)  to ensure transparency in network slicing operations.

"If they offer one or more slices to preferential users at a premium, it should not result in a degradation in ability of general internet users of the same service," said Anil Tandan, Director, BIF, at the same TRAI event. He further said that anybody should be allowed to partake in the slice if they wish to subscribe to that sliced network.

Network slicing can satisfy a wide range of use cases, said BIF: "5G systems will also provide an architectural flexibility and enable logical network slices. The term, Network Slicing which is in vogue to the 5G parties will enable operators to provide networks on an asa-service basis and satisfy the wide range of use cases," noted BIF in a 2018 [document](https://broadbandindiaforum.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Roadmap-for-5G-in-India-Online-14-MAY-18.pdf) titled Roadmap for 5G in India (PDF).  

Recommended read on how network slicing can be used to violate Net Neutrality: “Network slicing is by itself is not violative of Net Neutrality, but how the network is sliced is an issue: it can be neutral and enable access to all services equally in a slice, or it can violate net neutrality by offering application specific slicing and service-level slicing in different slices, and additionally, price different slices differentially,” wrote MediaNama editor Nikhil Pahwa. Read the full article here, where he points out how [Reliance Jio and GSMA nudge TRAI for Net Neutrality violating Network Slicing](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-jio-gsma-network-slicing-net-neutrality/)
