# Meta censors posts about censorship; SFLC claims keyword based censorship on Instagram

"Meta blocks SFLC post about Scroll report on Meta blocking SFLC post on Meta blocking Alt News posts." This [post](https://x.com/tajmahalfoxtrot/status/2101251902747066534?s=20) from Scroll.in's editor, Naresh Fernandes sums up everything.

[https://x.com/tajmahalfoxtrot/status/2101251902747066534?s=20](https://x.com/tajmahalfoxtrot/status/2101251902747066534?s=20) 

What actually happened? This is a part of ongoing and potentially automated online censorship in India. On September 19, Software Freedom law Center (SFLC) posted that their instagram post was amde “unavailable in India.” The Instagram notice, posted by SFLC stated that it was complying with the IT Rules. 

The SFLC’s post was citing the Scroll’s [report](https://www.instagram.com/p/Ddb11fjjVeW/) on censoring the Alt News’s report. The news report is about Meta blocking the Alt News’s posts. 

So, here are the very brief sequence of events: 

- Alt News’s post about the takedowns related to the Chauhaan film teaser, which had references to late Islamist militant, [Burhan Wani](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/other-states/%E2%80%98Burhan-Wani-Kashmir-Valley%E2%80%99s-most-wanted-militant-commander-killed%E2%80%99/article60528421.ece/amp/), was takendown by Meta. 
    
- SFLC made social media [post](https://x.com/SFLCin/status/2100843015069646969) condemning the online censorship. Meta blocked this post too. 
    
- Later, Scroll reported based on SFLC’s comments against censorship of Alt News’s reporting. (This is not blocked)
    
- SFLC then posted a [story](https://x.com/SFLCin/status/2101249522190221368?s=20), quoting the Scroll reporting. Meta blocked this too.
    

“Meta has imposed further restrictions on SFLC.in’s content. The platform's automated systems have restricted our Instagram story in India,” –  [SFLC](https://x.com/SFLCin/status/2101249522190221368)

SFLC’s claims of automated censorship were once confirmed by Aroon Deep’s The Hindu Report. He [reported](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-meta-instantly-blocks-content-india/) that India is one among a few countries where Meta “automatically blocks flagged content on its platforms.”

Keyword-based blocking? Addressing the blocking of AltNews’s post about BurhanWani, SFLC said that “Users in India can now see only the two slides that do not mention “Burhan Wani.”

“An Instagram post by Alt News based on the analysis of the teaser of the movie 'Chauhaan' was restricted by Instagram. A subsequent version was also removed, while a third version, after references to Hizbul Mujahideen militant W@n!" were removed, was allowed to remain online,” wrote SFLC in its [statement](https://www.instagram.com/p/Ddf6GimE5MF/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==). 

In its statement, SFLC also specified that Alt News stated that search results on Meta for "B*rh@n W@n!" on Instagram yielded no results and yet showed a message that said "these search terms may be associated with socially sensitive content.”

Nobody knows the exact reasons behind the arbitrary blocking: “Since the content has been taken down by automated systems it is unclear whether the restriction stems from a specific legal request or order issued to Meta, or whether Meta has restricted the content suo motu through its automated systems as part of its compliance with the due-diligence requirements under the Information Technology Rules,2021. SFLC.in has not been provided with sufficient information to determine the source of the restriction, the specific legal basis for it, or the process through which the decision can be challenged,” wrote [SFLC](https://www.instagram.com/p/Ddf6GimE5MF/?img_index=3). 

This is also an unfortunate standard practice these days. A few of the victicms of censorship had to approach the judiciary just to get the blocking order. 

For instance, when Environment Reporter Collective’s [Instagram reel was blocked in India](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-meta-blocks-environmental-reporting-collectives-instagram-reel-googles-ai-data-centers-ap/), they [approached Delhi HC](https://www.medianama.com/2026/07/223-inside-petition-journalists-challenge-blocking-instagram-reel-related-aps-ai-data-center/) to obtain the blocking order from Meta. They then approached the specific court on Andhra seeking restoration. Affer a few days, the [reel is restored without any written or verbal reason](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-instagram-reel-google-vizag-data-centre-restored-after-111-days-no-explanation-given/). No one knows exactly why the [Adani-Google AI data center reel highlighting the environmental concenrs](https://www.instagram.com/p/DYhWi_NzEL3/) was blocked and restored. Everything about online censorship in India has become arbitrary. 

“...opacity completely empowers people to censor speech without any restriction whatsoever and without any accountability whatsoever. That is expanding at a scale and pace.” – Nikhil Pahwa, MediaNama,
