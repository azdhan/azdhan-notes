**# Delhi HC orders Meta to remove AI deepfake images of a woman with PM Modi 

The Delhi High Court directed Meta to immediately remove morphed, AI-generated explicit images of a woman with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, allegedly created and circulated by activists of the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), and ordered police protection for her. 

Justice Girish Kathpalia took up the urgent criminal writ petition that accused CJP activists Saurav Das, Abhijeet Dipke, Ashutosh Ranka and Ratna Singh of morphing a woman’s images and circulating AI-generated pictures showing her with Prime Minister Narendra Modi. They told HC that a FIR has already been registered and that she now seeks removal of the images.

"This is extraordinary position in which the matter is not of one lady, it is matter about the prime minister of the country. If they are not sensitive, I will make sure that they are sensitive." – Justice Girish Kathpalia, Delhi HC

According to the court procedigs reported by Bar and Bench, she also sought reliefs from Meta  "to trace, block and purge her morphed explicity pictures and deepfake content not only from social media networks but from all pornographic websites, mirror domain and adult hosting platforms where the said pictures were illegally uploaded."

HC orders investigative agency to maintain confidentiality: To prevent the content from spreading, the Court directed the registry "to redact all those web links from the petition and the annexures as a matter of abundant precaution." When the petitioner  hands over all the pictures and videos, with their web links, to the investigating agency, the court ordered that the agency "shall maintain strict confidentiality of the said pictures and the videos."

Delhi Police should have taken suo moto cognizance, argues the petitioner: The Court directed the Delhi Police to file a status report within a week, "detailing the exact action taken." Sharma also told the Court that the Delhi Police has an in-house quick reaction team for such matters, and argued that since the material was in the public domain, "the police should have taken suo motu cognisance of this."

The Bench initially indicated that take down reliefs belonged in a civil court: "Taking down of pictures, for that the case has to go to civil court. I can grant police protection," said HC. Later the court also stated that “looking at the sensitivity, we should not make the lady run from one place to another." 

The Bench asked whether the petitioner had sought compensation from CJP: "have you sought compensation also? because i remember an order where it was said that whenver political parties are doing demonstrations etc and something happens, they will be liable to pay compensation." Sharma replied that no such relief had been sought. 

Delhi HC perhaps was refering to the Madras HC proceedings after Tamil Nadu CM Vijay Joseph's political rally in Karur that lead to the death of [41 individuals](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/tamil-nadu/a-year-after-karur-stampede-vijay-remembers-41-victims-unhealable-wound-4161681). After this incident, in September 2025, Justice N Sathish Kumar [observed](https://www.ndtv.com/chennai-news/no-one-above-law-court-orders-making-rules-for-rallies-by-political-parties-9302892), " "If something untoward had happened, who will take responsibility? As party president, Vijay ought to control the crowds." and asked whether the compensation has been collected.
