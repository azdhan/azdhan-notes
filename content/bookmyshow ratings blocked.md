**# BookMyShow disables reviews and ratings for another Telugu film citing a court order

BookMyShow has disabled ratings and reviews for the Telugu film [Aadarsha Kutumbam House No: 47 (AK 47)](https://in.bookmyshow.com/movies/hyderabad/aadarsha-kutumbam-house-no-47-ak-47/ET00475942), citing a court order. The film was released on October 30, 2026.

At least 14 films have had their ratings blocked on the ticketing platform since December 2025, according to MediaNama's count.  

The film's page carries a templated notice that reads: "Ratings & Reviews disabled as per court order." Users can only mark the film as "interested." Unlike BookMyShow, its rival competitor doesn’t have the ratings or reviews options. Users can only mark “hype,” an equivalent of traditional like or interested buttons.

The list shows the blocks did not stop after the Sankranti/Pongal season. The earliest title, The Devil, is dated more than a month before the January blocks MediaNama first reported, beginning with [Mana Shankara Vara Prasad Garu](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-bookmyshow-disables-reviews-telugu-film-court-order/) (MSG). It is not clear whether a court order was behind each block.

The difficulty in accessing the court orders: Whiile the public informationa available on BookMyShow’s website states that ‘a court order’ is behind the disable of reviews and ratings, the actual court order itself is not public and difficult to access for the journalists and the public to get access to the court orders that led to the censorship of reviews and ratings on BMS. 

MediaNama got its hands on the court order only because YouTuber Barbell shared the court order that was issued for [Mana Shankara Varaprasad Garu](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-bookmyshow-disables-reviews-telugu-film-court-order/), one of the very first big movies whose reviews and ratings were blocked. He got the court order because AIPlex company sent a legal notice to takedown his review.

“Even now, there are more than ten active cases against me. They keep filing in the same courts, routing them through Bangalore, and serving them to me one after another. My estimate for the end of this year? If it’s ten-plus now, I think we’re looking at 20 by December,”  – [YouTuber barbel, a.k.a Sushanth](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-telugu-producers-misusing-court-orders-curb-critical-online-reviews/). 

However, while doing the research for this current article we found that there were atleast three more Kannada movies who procured blocking orders for the movies released in December 2026. 

What the earlier order said:

In January, MediaNama [reported](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-bookmyshow-ratings-blocked-telugu-films/) that an order from Bengaluru's City Civil Court was behind the blocks on [three more Telugu films](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-bookmyshow-ratings-blocked-telugu-films/). The ad-interim, ex parte injunction named platforms including BookMyShow and IMDb. It directed them to block "defamatory or orchestrated negative responses or ratings". It also barred reaction videos, community polls and boycott campaigns.

The court orders are not public. MediaNama accessed the details through YouTuber Sushanth, whose channel received a legal notice from AiPlex. He then [made his review video private](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xa2dE7ACds). It is not clear whether the block on AK 47 rests on this order, an extension of it, or a new one.

In Janurary 2026, Trade analyst Taran Adarsh [claimed](https://x.com/taran_adarsh/status/2010330103134425180) that [BlockBigg](https://www.blockbigg.com/) and [AiPlex](https://aiplex.com/), working with the Bharat Digital Media Federation, helped obtain the January order for Telugu film mana Vara Shankara Prasad Garu. AiPlex describes itself as an anti-piracy and online reputation management service provider. BlockBigg describes itself as a narrative control and reputation management service.

Why producers are getting the blocking orders: 

Producer Naga Vamsi, who is also the producer for Aadarsha Kutumbam House No: 47 (AK 47), [said](https://x.com/i/status/2011385931740430561) in an interview with pressmeet that his team obtained the January order to preserve its legal right to act against disrespectful reviews, not to stop reviewers.

In a separate interview with GreatAndhra, he [said](https://youtu.be/OXwsADX6gfk?si=oBa4T20r0zJnAgRr&t=1532) producers have paid for likes and ratings on BookMyShow, a claim the platform denied and claimed 

“All the likes and ratings you see on BookMyShow, we have been spending money on them. A PRO comes and says that two films are releasing together, but their film has more likes on BookMyShow, and ours has fewer, so we have to do it [paid likes and ratings],” - [Naga Vamsi Telugu film producer.](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-bookmyshow-disables-reviews-telugu-film-court-order/) 

In January 2026, BookMyShow told MediaNama earlier that it is "a law-abiding intermediary platform and strictly complies with all applicable laws and court orders." It also said it is not involved in paid, fake or manipulated ratings.  

What Indian courts have said about online movie reviews: 

In June 2025, the Madras High Court [ruled](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/banning-online-film-reviews-equivalent-to-curtailing-fundamental-right-to-freedom-of-speech-and-expression-madras-high-court/article69740119.ece) that banning online film reviews would interfere with the fundamental right to freedom of speech and expression. Tamil producers had sought a three-day ban on reviews after a film's release.

Similarly, in 2024, a Kerala HC-appointed amicus curiae [recommended guidelines](https://www.medianama.com/2024/03/223-kerala-hc-expert-advisor-call-guidelines-influencer-movie-reviews/) for influencer-led movie reviews. Meanwhile, in 2023, the Kerala High Court [examined review bombing](https://www.medianama.com/2023/10/223-review-bombing-kerala-high-court/) in October 2023 and asked the state police to suggest ways to curb malicious reviews.
