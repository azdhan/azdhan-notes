
# policy design of indian elections 
**smartphones are not allowed inside the polling booths** because voters can take the photo of whom they have voted and they ask for the money/liquor from the party they have voted because it becomes a verifiable asset. the intent here is: wherever there is a scope for the coercion, the policy's attempts should be to prevent the coercion from happening. 

**votes shouldn't become the verifiable assets** because that leads to the identification of the person too. and, in a democracy anonymous voting is the only the independent choice can be measured. and, if the lawmakers want to bring in the internet-based voting, that would take the anonymity out of scope and that shouldn't happen.
