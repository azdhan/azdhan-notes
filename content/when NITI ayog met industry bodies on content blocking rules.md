# NITI Aayog met with intermediaries and industry bodies on content blocking: Report

On June 25, NITI Ayog reportedly held a meeting where representatives from big tech intermediaries and a few industry bodies have participated in a closed-door meeting and discussed India's current online content blocking requirements and transparency timelines, reported[ Indian Express'](https://indianexpress.com/article/business/niti-seeks-tech-firms-views-on-content-blocking-regime-10791892/)  Soumyarendra Barik. 

**A couple questions NITI Aayog apparently asked during their discussion:**
- “Are current takedown, grievance, and transparency timelines operationally feasible across different categories and sizes of intermediaries?” 
- “Which intermediary due diligence obligations impose the highest recurring compliance burden, and what specific operational simplifications would be most impactful?”

**Representatives involved in that discussion were:**
- **Intermediaries like:** 
	- Facebook, 
	- Instagram, and 
	- YouTube.
- **Industry bodies like:** 
	- Nasscom, 
	- Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), 
	- Internet and Mobile Association of India (IAMAI), and 
	- Broadband India Forum (BIF), among others 

The topmost public policy think tank of the Government of India also sought views on a wide range of 'regulatory issues, covering laws impacting social media companies and other online intermediaries, cybersecurity, data protection, and online gaming, among others,' reported IE.

These discussions are reportedly happening under the NITI Aayog’s new ‘Jan Vishwas Siddhant’ initiative, which may be behind Ministry of  
Commerce and Industry's proposed The Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill, 2026 that aims to 'amend 80 central Acts to decriminalise or rationalise offences and penalties.' Read more about it [here](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-jan-vishwas-amendment-of-provisions-bill-2026). 

Based on such discussions NITI Aayog might submit their report with MeitY.
