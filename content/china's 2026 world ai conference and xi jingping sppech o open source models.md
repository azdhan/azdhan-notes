
# China's Xi Jinping announces World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization; calls for open source AI models 

China announced the Shanghai-based World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO) with 29 countries, which include Pakistan, Russia and Indonesia among nations. China made this announcement as it was starting its 15th Five-Year Plan, from July 17 to 20, China held  World AI Conference (2026), an annual conference that China has been hosting [since](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/explainer-what-to-expect-from-china-s-global-ai-conference/4000542#:~:text=hosted%20WAIC%20annually-,since,-2018.) 2018-- four years prior to the launch of ChatGPT. 

Here are a few important statements made by CCP President Xi Jinping in his key note address:

**Calls for open source, openness, collaboration and sharing AI models:** "We should adhere to the principle of openness and win-win and boost innovation-driven development... We should seize this rare, historic opportunity to encourage open source, openness, collaboration and sharing. We should facilitate technological innovation, industrial development and scenario-based application of AI," he said. He also called for AI innovations in the traditionals and forward-looking industries to ensure AI benefits to everyone. 

**Calls for monitoring and warning systems for security and to prevent abuse:** "We should strengthen risk-awareness and ensure that AI is secure and controllable.... We should put in place laws and regulations, technological monitoring, early warning and emergency response systems in order to strengthen the line of security, prevent abuses and malicious use, and ensure that AI is always under human control."

**Calls for multilateral global cooperation to bridge AI digital divides:** "We should practice true multilateralism and recognize the important role of the United Nations. We should enhance alignment and coordination on AI development strategies, governance rules and technical standards, so as to form a consensus-based global governance framework at an early date to make this frontier technology better benefit humanity. We must carry out extensive international cooperation and help Global South countries with capacity building to bridge the AI and digital divides, promote sustainable development, and prevent creating new historical injustice in AI."

**What is  World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO)?** According to the news reports by China's state-run media [Xinhua News,](https://english.news.cn/20260716/2d364f232dd7456c8edf1d67d5125d37/c.html) WAICO will be a Shanghai-based 'independent intergovernmental international organization' with 29 countries as their members. 'It aims to promote international cooperation and global governance on AI, ensuring that AI is beneficial, safe and fair, thereby promoting its healthy and orderly development to benefit all humanity,' reads their news report. Read their press release [here](http://en.cppcc.gov.cn/2026-07/20/c_1198497.htm).

He further added that
- China has promoted the adoption of the U.N. General Assembly Resolution on Enhancing International Cooperation on Capacity-Building of Artificial Intelligence since he [proposed](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202407/02/content_WS668394a7c6d0868f4e8e8c58.html) Global AI governance initiative. Read more about 2024's UN resolution [here](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4054005?ln=en&v=pdf). 
- China published the AI Capacity-Building Action Plan for Good and for All. Read 2024's China plan [here]([AI Capacity-Building Action Plan for Good and for All_Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/eng/wjbzhd/202409/t20240927_11498465.html)). 
- And, announced the AI Plus International Cooperation Initiative. Read more about it [here](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202607/17/content_WS6a5a1bbec6d00ca5f9a0c474.html). 
- Advocated for establishing the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO), which was signed during the current annual AI conference. 


At the 2026 World AI Conference and High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance in Shanghai, running from July 17 to 20, exhibitors showcased a wide range of AI agent hardware and systems aimed at demonstrating AI's shift from a tool to an "intelligent partner" across industries and daily life.

Other exhibits featured:
- non-invasive brain-computer interface device used to play a computer game, 
- AI glasses with payment functionality, 
- service robots,

- iFlytek's AI-powered blackboard,
