does these policies makes any sense when compared with the business implementations? 

**how water is utilised at data centres?** as the systems process the data, they require cooling infrastructure and water is the coolant that is popularly used (why only water?) there are two types broadly: 
- closed loop systems - water is sprayed into the air to cool the systems. requires more water. 
- open loop systems - refridgerated after is passed through the pipes. requires more electricity. industry is moving in this category these days. 

how is cooling infra different from the servers? 
in servers, it is at the microprocessor level, chip cooling liquids/fluids are generally used. this is micro in comparison with the data center coolant process.
