##  Gujarat Police has flagged one URL  each 6 minutes.

**

  RTI: Gujarat Police flagged one URL every six minutes through Sahyog portal

On average, one URL was being flagged per every ~6 minutes by the Gujrat police through the Center’s censorship facilitaror Sahyog Portal since its inception in October 2024, according to the Right to Information.(RTI) reply shared with MediaNama. The person who has filed the RTI application has requested anonymity. 

According to the RTI reply MediaNama reviewed, Gujarat Police issued 5,358 takedown notices, covering 190,633 URLs, to “all intermediaries” through the Centre's Sahyog portal over the past two years, possibly since October 2024, when Sahyog portal was launched by the Ministry of Home Affairs. The reply does not give a cut-off date for "to date" and the reply was received on October 28, 2026.  

It means, on average:

- ~261 URLs per day that amounts to roughly one URL flagged every six minutes 
    
- ~6.75 notices per day. 
    
- ~36 URLs listed in each notice. 
    

Dated October 28, the RTI reply is a result of a second appeal which came from the Cyber Centre of Excellence, which sits in the Office of the Inspector General of Police, Gujarat. 

What was asked:

The RTI application asked the Home Department, Gujarat to disclose the total number of takedown notices issued to intermediaries under Section 79(3)(b) of the IT Act or Rule 3(1)(d) of the IT Rules, 2021, including copies, and specifically how many of those were sent through the Sahyog Portal.

The application was filed with the Gujarat Home Department on June 17, 2026, transferred to the Cyber Centre of Excellence by a First Appellate Authority order, and was finally answered on October 28, 2026.

Center’s Sahyog Portal itself doesn’t hold takedown records, reveals another RTI reply

In another [RTI reply to MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-i4c-sahyog-portal-takedown-records-rti/), the Ministry of Home Affairs’s Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), which runs the portal, said that it does not hold records of notices issued. I4C said this data sits with State and Union Territory police. Gujarat has now disclosed an aggregate figure, without citing any RTI exemption. 

This could mean that each individual state police department should also disclose the number of figures, number of takedown notices that have been issued using their Sahyog portal. MediaNama will file RTI applications with each state police department and will publish copies if and when they respond. 

Brief context on Sahyog Portal:

MHA’s I4C [created the portal](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-explained-what-is-the-sahyog-portal-that-x-called-out-for-censorship/) to automate Section 79(3)(b) notices. In a submission to the Karnataka High Court, the government said the portal shows each agency's total requests, content taken down, and closed and pending requests. 

“As per screenshots attached with the Government’s response, the portal contains a dashboard which shows Central ministries/departments along with States, and the total number of requests they have sent out. It also shows them the total pieces of content that platforms have taken down, the number of requests closed and the number of requests pending. The dashboard also provides a platform-wide breakdown of requests and the status of said requests.

Besides this, agencies have the option to generate a takedown notice and simply select the Act/Section under which a piece of content is unlawful.” – [MediaNama reporting on Sahyog Portal](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-explained-what-is-the-sahyog-portal-that-x-called-out-for-censorship/)

[X challenged the portal](https://www.medianama.com/2025/03/223-x-vs-indian-government-key-points-x-petition-against-sahyog-portal/), arguing that the government uses it to bypass the safeguards in [Section 69A](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/show-data?actid=AC_CEN_45_76_00001_200021_1517807324077&orderno=89), the provision meant for blocking orders. The Karnataka High Court [upheld the portal](https://www.medianama.com/2025/09/223-why-karnataka-hc-sahyog-portal-x-allegations/). The Supreme Court has since [stayed all previous High Court judgments](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-supreme-court-stays-sahyog-portal-cases/) in the case.
