# DNPA meeting with MEITY on DPDP Act 
[[DNPA against the DPDP act at SC]]
Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, please provide the following information held by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).

Reuters reported on 26 October 2026 (https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/ambani-adani-outlets-among-indian-publishers-contesting-modis-privacy-law-2026-10-26/) that the Digital News Publishers Association (DNPA) referred in its Supreme Court petition to an August 2026 meeting with the government concerning the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and press freedom, and attached an August letter to the government. This request concerns MeitY's records of that consultation, for 1 January 2025 to 26 October 2026.

1. Copies of MeitY-held meeting invitations, agendas and attendance records for any meeting in August within that period between MeitY and DNPA or its representatives concerning the Act's application to journalism or press freedom.
2. Copies of minutes, meeting notes, briefing papers and records of any assurances or commitments recorded by MeitY for each such meeting. If no formal minutes were prepared, please provide any contemporaneous record of the discussion held by MeitY.
3. Copies of letters, representations and emails received by MeitY from DNPA on this subject during that period, including the August letter if received, and MeitY's replies, if any.
4. Copies of MeitY file notings, recorded decisions and correspondence on DNPA's request for protections or exemptions for journalistic activity under the Act or the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, during that period.

Please provide records not marked confidential. Personal identifiers and any exempt third-party commercial details may be redacted under Section 10; please supply the remaining portions. There is a larger public interest under Section 8(2) in disclosure of MeitY's handling of representations about press freedom under the data protection law, which outweighs any claimed commercial interest. The request is for MeitY's own records, not information held in trust for a third party.

If another public authority holds any part, kindly transfer that part under Section 6(3) within five days. Kindly send the reply by email. If an additional fee is due, please raise it through the RTI MIS additional-fee option for online payment. If any part is denied, kindly cite the specific clause of Section 8 or 9 relied upon and give reasons as required by Section 7(8).

Copies of the posting, appointment, transfer or relieving orders recording each officer’s assumption and cessation of charge as Director of the Information Security Division (or an earlier equivalent designation), including the officer holding that post on 25 October 2026. 3.
