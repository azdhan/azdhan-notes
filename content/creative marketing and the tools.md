# product placement marketing in cinema

When a fruit juice product was accidentally featured in a film called *Anukokunda Oka Roju*, the protagonist Jagapathi Babu is seen drinking it. However, when the company [approached](https://x.com/Attipanduthatha/status/2074736840502849885?s=20) the filmmakers after the shoot to ask for marketing money, they were told that the package labeling had been changed and that they couldn't pay. This reminds me of the product placement—I think it was a Nokia mobile phone—in the movie *Chennai Express*. These are very smart ways to plug a product into films. I think it's happening more often, and I've seen a couple of such placements in Telugu cinema as well.
