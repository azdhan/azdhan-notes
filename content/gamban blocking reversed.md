# Section 69A blocking order revered: MeitY unblocks Gamban after Inter-ministerial hearing

After 3 years and 8 months, the Union IT ministry has unblocked Gamban, a UK-based app that blocks gambling sites and apps for people recovering from gambling addiction, whose website was blocked in India, while the app was taken down from app stores.

The Ministry of Electronics and IT (MeitY) had blocked the app from Google Playstore and Apple Playstore and its website under Section 69A of the Information Technology (IT) Act since February 7, 2023. MeitY communicated its decision to Gamban on November 2, via e-mail, according to Jay Sayta, one of Gamban's advocates who represented Gamban at Delhi HC and at the Inter-ministerial committee hearing. 

The inter-ministerial committee hearing and the unblocking of Gamban website followed the [Delhi High Court direction to MeitY to decide within four weeks](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-delhi-hc-meity-gamban-blocking-order/). Gamban welcomed the unblocking of the website and [thanked](https://x.com/gambanapp/status/2117162826045280464) the Indian government. 

Section 69A orders are almost never reversed: In August 2022, MediaNama [noted](https://www.medianama.com/2022/08/223-meity-review-committee-not-one-69a-blocking-order-revoked-3/) that Not a single Section 69A blocking order issued by the government since 2009 has been revoked by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology’s (MeitY) Review Committee, according to the RTI filled by now Crockroach Janta Party’s official spokesperson Saurav Das. 

Jay also described unblocking under Section 69A a rare case, saying he has "seen very less unblocking," and that this outcome is "one of the very few examples where this has actually led to the unblocking." 

[MeitY treats them as Section 69A oders as confidential](https://www.medianama.com/2021/05/223-meity-section-69a-confidential/). Jay also specified that MeitY hasn’t shared the blocking order with them. 

Apple is yet to unblock: internet service providers (ISPs) appear to have already unblocked the website and the [website](https://gamban.com/) remains accessible on both Airtel and Jio networks. While [Google's Play Store has restored the app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gamban.beanstalkhps.gambanapp&hl=en_IN) and Apple's App Store have not yet restored the app. Jay said that Gamban will send the ministry's decision to both app store intermediaries and ask them to act. However, Just a few hours after our call with him, the App became accessible on Google Play Store.

While the Blocking Rules require an intermediary to acknowledge a blocking direction "within two hours on receipt of the direction" under the Rule 13(2), Jay argued they set no deadline for acting in an unblocking direction. "It is a matter of time," he said. 

What happened at the inter-ministerial hearing: 

  

In an interview with MediaNama, Advocate Jay explained what happened at the Inter-ministrial hearing that happened on October 14.

After [Delhi HC directed Meit](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-delhi-hc-meity-gamban-blocking-order/)Y to decide on Gamban's petition within four weeks, Jay said that Gamban then wrote to MeitY seeking a hearing. According to Jay, MeitY told Gamban on October 13 that an inter-ministerial committee would meet on October 14, and invited written and oral submissions via online conference.

"We submitted our written representation. And, I personally attended and represented Gamban at the hearing," Jay said. He also added that Gamban's representatives also joined him to answer technical questions, if the Committee had any. 

He described the meeting as "cordial," noting that the officers heard them patiently and raised their concerns, which the company addressed.

Jay said the ministry raised two concerns, which also echoes the allegations in MeitY's [May 6, 2025 email](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-meitys-section-69a-blocked-anti-gambling-app-gamban-3-years-no-reasons-shared/) to Gamban:

- whether the app collects financial or other sensitive user data and 
    
- whether uninstalling it increases the gambling ads a user sees. 
    

Gamban's reply, as Jay described it, was that the app reads only the address bar to match a URL against its list of gambling domains. It takes no other data and shares none with third parties. The company also submitted an International Organization for Standardization (ISO) certificate on privacy. 

After 14 days of the initial hearing, the Inter ministerial Committee held another meeting on October 28 where it had recommended the unblocking of Gamban website and thereby directed the concerned intermediary app stores to unblock Gamban. 

Beyond this, what is discussed at the Committee was not disclosed as the Rule 16 of the blocking Rules mandates the “Strict confidentiality shall be maintained regarding all the requests and complaints received and actions taken thereof.” 

  

Who sat on the Inter-Ministerial committee: 

  

According to the [Rule 7 of the Information Technology (Procedure and Safeguards for Blocking for Access of Information by Public) Rules, 2009,](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/10/91f628cb778f94e76df356bc3fd3ac60.pdf) the committee's composition should be:

- The Designated Officer, appointed by MietY, as the chairperson of the committee,
    
- A representative of the Ministry of Law and Justice
    
- A representative of the Ministry of Home Affairs
    
- A representative of the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting
    
- A representative of the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)
    

"The request alongwith the printed sample content of the alleged offending information or part thereof shall be examined by a committee consisting of the Designated Officer as its chairperson and representatives, not below the rank of Joint Secretary in Ministries of Law and Justice, Home Affairs, Information and Broadcasting and the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team appointed under sub-section (1) of section 70B of the Act." - [Rule 7 of Blocking Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/10/91f628cb778f94e76df356bc3fd3ac60.pdf)

Jay said the officer who engaged with Gamban at the Committee’s hearing was a MeitY joint secretary, the Section 69A Designated Officer. One other MeitY official also took part. He said the representatives of the other ministries were listed online but did not appear on video. Gamban was not told their names.  

Jay said representatives of the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), the nodal agency for the complaint, were also present. For context, during the court proceedings, MeitY's counsel had [told the Delhi HC](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-delhi-hc-meity-gamban-blocking-order/) that I4C was the nodal agency that issued the blocking order against Gamban.  

According to [MeitY's October 2025’s notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/e962fe0e54b04e82dab3b9d6753a2c3b.pdf), Sushil Pal, the Joint Secretary of MeitY, and Akil Kumar, the Managing Director/ Chief Executive Officer, Digital India Corporation were appointed as the Designated Officer and S Krishnan is the current MeitY’s Secretary. 

What is the due process after the Inter-Ministerial hearing?

The committee itself does not decide whether a site stays blocked as it only has the specific recommendation powers under the [Blocking Rules’s Rule 8(4),](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/10/91f628cb778f94e76df356bc3fd3ac60.pdf) 

Under Rule 8(5), the Designated Officer sends that recommendation, along with the details from the nodal officer, to the Secretary in the Department of Information Technology. Then, MeitY’s secretary takes the decision.

Rule 8(6) then splits the process in two:

- If the Secretary approves: the Designated Officer directs the government agency or intermediary to block/unblock the information for public access, within the time limit in the direction.
    
- If the Secretary does not approve: the Designated Officer informs the nodal officer that the request was not approved.
