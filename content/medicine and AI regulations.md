**Ministry:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)

**Overview:** National policy roadmap outlining guidelines for the safe, ethical, and responsible integration of AI into India's healthcare system.

## 2. Benchmarking Open Data Platform for Health AI (BODH, March 2026)

**Ministry:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)

**Overview:** Announced during the [[India AI Impact Summit 2026]], BODH serves as "a structured mechanism for testing and validating AI solutions before deployment at scale." Benchmarks published in academic journal in March 2026.

## 3. Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020)

**Prepared by:** Medical Council of India in partnership with NITI Aayog

**Key restrictions:**

- AI and machine learning platforms are **not permitted** to counsel patients or prescribe medicines
- Only registered medical practitioners can provide counseling and prescriptions
- AI, IoT, and data science-based decision support systems may assist in patient evaluation, diagnosis, or management
- Final prescription and patient counselling must be provided directly by a doctor

**Resources:**

- [Original PDF](https://www.mohfw.gov.in/pdf/Telemedicine.pdf)
- [Archived PDF](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Telemedicine-Practice-Guidelines.pdf)

## 4. Draft Guidelines for Medical Device Software (November 2026)

**Ministry:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) via CDSCO

**Timeline:** Released October 2026; 30-day public consultation period

**Overview:** Aims to align India's regulatory framework with global standards and clarify requirements for AI, digital, and cloud-based medical software.

**Coverage:**

- Scope and classification
- Technical requirements
- Quality management systems for medical device software
