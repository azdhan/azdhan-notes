# AU Small Finance Bank probes a ‘potential data leak’ 

As Indian financial institutions are facing [multiple cyberattacks](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-ai-cyberattacks-siphon-rs-300-crore-nbfcs/), AU Small Finance Bank is investigating a potential data leak on a Salesforce-hosted platform that holds data on its vehicle loan customers, the bank said in an e-mail response to MediaNama after a hacking group claimed on the dark web that it had breached the lender. Cyber threat intelligence account [Hackmanac](https://x.com/H4ckmanac/status/2115790335318098295) first reported the claim on X.

The bank also calims that its own systems are unaffected. Here is the full statement shared with MediaNama:

"AU Small Finance Bank is investigating a potential data leak involving a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform managed and hosted by Salesforce, which holds limited data relating to our vehicle loan customers."

"We reaffirm that the Bank's own systems remain unaffected and all banking services are operating normally." – AU Small Finance Bank 

MediaNama had reviewed the hacker’s website on dark web and the hackers' listing describes Aadhaar and PAN details, credit reports, signed loan agreements and employee records, which is far broader than the "limited data" the bank has acknowledged. However, the hacker hasn’t released either samples or the full datasets in public. The website currently states this as a “negotiation” state with a deadline countdown of five days 17 hours at the time of writing this. 

  

MediaNama could not independently verify that the group holds any data. We are not naming the group or linking to its website, to avoid further exploitation of any data that may have leaked.

What the listing describes

The listing groups the claimed data into categories. We reproduce the description as it appears, from an OCR (text extracted from a screenshot) of the page:

"Customer KYC (Core): Per details (name, DOB, PAN, Aadhaar, contact information, family, address, income, education, risk categorq), photos, signatures, CIF/API payloads, field verification notes, and KYC screening records (CKYC. CFR, ICU)."

"Credit and Financial Records: CIBIL reports, FICO scores, address checks, bank statements,_Aadhaar tokens, bureau data, applicant financials, loan applications, approvals, collateral, insurance, disbursements, paqment schedules, and account statements."

"Signed Loan Documents: e5ign PDFs, loan applications, KFS, sanction letters, MACH mandates, collateral agreements, insurance forms, and supporting 'documents."

"cRrei Person Records: Customer contacts, accounts, addresses, case notes, queries, assignments, work orders, bureau responses, and stored files (ContentVersion)."

"Stacc a: Internal Identity: Employee directory, contact details, permissions, group memberships, account teams, roles, login history, IF' addresses, and audit logs.  
  
and much more." –  Description of alleged hacked data of AU small finance bank. 

The listing also claims employee directories, permissions and audit logs, which the bank has not addressed. The bank's emphasis on its "own systems" does not answer whether customer data was exposed through a third-party platform.
